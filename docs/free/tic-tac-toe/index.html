Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-Tac-Toe Variants

Classic

X and O on a 3×3 grid — line up three to win.

Duck

Place your mark, then move the duck to block your opponent.

Notakto

Everyone plays X — complete a row of three and you lose.

Reverse

Misere rules: avoid making three in a row at all costs.

T3

Max three marks each — the fourth erases your oldest.

Wild

Pick X or O every turn — either mark can win you the game.
