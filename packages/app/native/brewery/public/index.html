Overview

A clean SwiftUI wrapper around brew — discover, install, upgrade, and manage formulae, casks, and services without touching the terminal.

Search & install

Browse formulae and casks, read their details, and install with one click.

Update everything

Refresh Homebrew metadata and upgrade every outdated package at once.

Services at hand

List Homebrew services and start, stop, or restart each one from the sidebar.

Upgrade & uninstall

See what's installed with full details, then upgrade or uninstall as needed.
