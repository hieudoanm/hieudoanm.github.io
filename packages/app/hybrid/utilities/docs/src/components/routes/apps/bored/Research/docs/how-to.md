## Overview

This app generates a random concept from **450 niches across 15 categories** —
from Acoustics to Criminal Law, Organic Chemistry to Metaphysics. Once you land
on a topic, use these prompt templates to dive deeper with AI.

## Prompt Templates

Pick any template, replace `{topic}` with your landed concept, and paste into
your AI of choice.

### Explain Like I'm 15

```txt
Explain "{topic}" to me like I'm 15 years old.
Use analogies and everyday examples. Keep it under 3 paragraphs.
```

### Deep Dive

```txt
I want to learn about "{topic}" in depth.
Cover:
- Core concepts and definitions
- Key milestones or discoveries
- Current state of the field
- Open questions or controversies
- Practical applications
```

### Debate / Counterarguments

```txt
What are the strongest arguments for and against "{topic}"?
Present both sides fairly, then give your assessment.
```

### Teach Me Step by Step

```txt
Teach me "{topic}" step by step as if I'm a complete beginner.
Start with prerequisites, then build up to the key ideas.
Include a simple exercise to test my understanding.
```

### Historical Context

```txt
What is the history behind "{topic}"?
Who were the key figures, what were the major turning points,
and how has the understanding evolved over time?
```

### Socratic Dialogue

```txt
Lead me through a Socratic dialogue about "{topic}".
Ask me a series of questions that help me arrive at the key insights myself.
Respond to my answers before moving to the next question.
```

### Compare and Contrast

```txt
Compare and contrast "{topic}" with something similar but distinct.
Highlight the key differences, overlaps, and edge cases.
Help me understand why the distinction matters.
```

### Real-World Application

```txt
Give me 5 real-world examples or applications of "{topic}".
For each one, explain how it connects to the core idea
and why it matters in practice.
```

### Research Paper Summary

```txt
Summarise a typical research paper on "{topic}" in 5 sections:
1. Research question
2. Methodology
3. Key findings
4. Limitations
5. Future work
Use hypothetical but realistic details.
```

### Cross-Disciplinary Connections

```txt
How does "{topic}" connect to other fields?
Show me at least 3 unexpected links between this concept
and completely different disciplines.
```

## Example

**Landed topic:** _Standing wave_

Paste this into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini:

```txt
Explain "Standing wave" to me like I'm 15 years old.
Use analogies and everyday examples. Keep it under 3 paragraphs.
```

Then follow up with:

```txt
Give me 5 real-world examples or applications of "Standing wave".
```

## Tips

- **Start broad, then narrow.** Use the _Explain Like I'm 15_ template first,
  then follow up with a deeper template once you have context.
- **Ask for sources.** Add _"Include sources or further reading"_ to any prompt.
- **Challenge the AI.** Use the _Debate_ template to stress-test ideas.
- **Chain prompts.** Use the output of one prompt as input for the next.
- **Copy-paste the topic exactly.** Topics are short concept phrases — they work
  best when quoted verbatim.
