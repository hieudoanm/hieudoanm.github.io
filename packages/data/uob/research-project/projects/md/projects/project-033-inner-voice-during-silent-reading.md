## Project title

Inner voice during silent reading

## Project summary

Most people experience some degree of inner voice during reading, i.e., they "hear" themselves while reading. However, whether this is just an epiphenomenon of how we learn to read (we start with reading aloud and then gradually internalise the sounds) or whether this is an essential component of skilled reading is unclear. We will test whether the degree one uses their inner voice impacts on reading fluency and comprehensions.
