## Project title

More or Less: Are the General Factor of Personality and HEXACO Models Valid?

## Project summary

A considerable body of research has been conducted into the Five Factor Model of personality (AKA the 'Big 5'), which consists of the traits, Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism. Increasingly there is evidence for the validity of competing personality models such as the HEXACO personality model and the General Factor of Personality (GFP). Nonetheless, many personality researchers dispute the validity of the GFP, in particular, and argue that it is the erroneous product of socially desirable responding and an artifact of factor analysis. However, research has revealed that this personality factor remains readily detectable once socially desirable responding has been controlled for, and there is now good evidence of its criterion and predictive validity. I would be able to supervise thesis projects on the validity of the HEXACO personality model, or in which the validity of the GFP is tested while socially desirable responding is controlled for.
