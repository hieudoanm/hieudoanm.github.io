## Project title

Decoding Speech, Meaning, and Narrative from the Human Brain

## Project summary

Recent advances in neural decoding and large language models (LLMs) have opened new possibilities for understanding how speech, meaning, and higher-level linguistic information are represented in the brain. In this project, we aim to investigate neural language representations during both naturalistic movie viewing and internally generated, imagined speech. Using non-invasive neuroimaging techniques such as MEG and OPM-MEG, together with computational modelling and LLMs, we will examine how the brain tracks and integrates speech, semantic meaning, and broader narrative context, and whether these representations can be decoded from brain activity. By linking naturalistic language comprehension with internally generated speech, this work aims to advance our understanding of the neural mechanisms of language and to contribute to the development of non-invasive neural decoding approaches with potential applications in brain–computer interfaces.
