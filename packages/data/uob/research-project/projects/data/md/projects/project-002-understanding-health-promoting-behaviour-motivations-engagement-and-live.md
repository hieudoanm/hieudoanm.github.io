## Project title

Understanding health promoting behaviour: Motivations, Engagement and Lived Experiences

## Project summary

This qualitative project aims to understand what motivates adults to engage in healthy behaviour and how perceived facilitators and barriers may influence engagement in, and experience of, health promoting behaviour. There will be substantial scope for each student to personalise their project. For example, you could focus on physical health, psychological health or take a more holistic approach to explore health more generally. You can also choose which behaviours you would like to focus on (e.g. team sports, individual exercise, sleep hygiene, healthy eating, self-care, help-seeking, meditation, mindfulness etc) and which methodologies (e.g. semi-structured interviews or focus groups ) and analyses (e.g. thematic analysis, IPA or discourse analysis) you will use.
