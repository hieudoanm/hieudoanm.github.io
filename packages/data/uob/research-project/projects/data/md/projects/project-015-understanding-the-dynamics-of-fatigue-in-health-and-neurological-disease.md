## Project title

Understanding the dynamics of fatigue in health and neurological disease.

## Project summary

Fatigue -a pervasive feeling of exhaustion - is a sensation we are all familiar with. However, in many neurological conditions such as Parkinson's Disease and Multiple Sclerosis, feelings of fatigue can become severe and pervasive, significantly impairing daily life. Yet, relative to many other symptoms in neurology, we know very little about what in the brain and body underlies the symptom of fatigue. Recently, research led by the supervisor has begun to characterise moment-to-moment how fatigue rises and falls during demanding tasks, developing experimental tools that can precisely measure for each individual how rapidly fatigue builds up, and how quickly it subsides through rest.

The aim of this project will be to develop a new experiment that measures how much exerting effort, or taking rests, impacts on sensations of fatigue, and the degree to which it modulates levels of motivation. We will then use computational modelling to unpack the brain mechanisms underlying changes in fatigue and motivation and to quantify variability between people to test its association with levels of fatigue in daily life in the healthy population. By developing this new approach, it will provide a platform for testing how fatigue fluctuates in neurological disorders.
