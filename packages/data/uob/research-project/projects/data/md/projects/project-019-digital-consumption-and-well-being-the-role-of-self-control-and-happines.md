## Project title

Digital Consumption and Well-Being: The Role of Self-Control and Happiness

## Project summary

Generation Z is highly familiar with technology and social media, yet research indicates that increased time on digital devices is associated with lower levels of happiness. This may be because digital media displaces more fulfilling activities or directly undermines well-being by fostering negative social comparisons. Excessive smartphone use has also been shown to reduce face-to-face social interactions. While the relationship between internet use and happiness remains inconclusive, higher levels of social media use are consistently linked to lower attentional control. Given that self-control supports the regulation of attention, it may serve as a protective factor against excessive digital consumption. This raises a key question: to what extent do self-control and happiness predict patterns of social media use?

## Topic

The relationship between self-control, happiness, and social media use among Generation Z
