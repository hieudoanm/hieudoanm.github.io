## Project title

Developing resources for feedback literacy in Higher Education

## Project summary

Assessment and feedback are notoriously difficult aspects of students' Higher Education experiences, often associated with strong emotions. This project aims to draw on students insights into the assessment and feedback processes to start developing resources that might help students get the most out of their assessment and feedback opportunities.

## Topic

assessment and feedback in HE, learning, optimum functioning, student experience

## Methodology

May include: mixed methods - qualitative interviews and analysis, questionnaire/survey design, experimental testing of newly designed resources
