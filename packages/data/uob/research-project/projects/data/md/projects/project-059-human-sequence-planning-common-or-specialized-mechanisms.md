## Project title

Human sequence planning:  Common or specialized mechanisms?

## Project summary

Planning and carrying out sequences is a common in many human domains:  Memory, language, music, movement.  Will will have participants perform sequences in several different domains and test whether underlying mechanisms for producing sequences are common or specialized.  We will look at memory sequences, word and/or sentence production and movement sequences (we may be able to customize the domains to some extent)

## Topic

memory, language, motor control

## Methodology

Cognitive and/or motor testing and RT and/or error analysis
