## Project title

Cognitive development in monolingual and multilingual toddlers born preterm

## Project summary

More than 1 in 10 births are preterm (<37 weeks gestational age) and more than 50% of the world's population is multilingual. Children born preterm have shown slower language and slower cognitive development than their full-term peers. In turn, multilingualism slows down language development in both languages, while seemingly accelerating cognitive development. The current project investigates these developments more thoroughly. Students are asked to develop their questions within this larger project.
