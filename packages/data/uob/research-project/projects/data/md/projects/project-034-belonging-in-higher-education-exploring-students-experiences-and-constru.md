## Project title

Belonging in Higher Education: Exploring Students' Experiences and Constructions Through an Intersectional Lens

## Project summary

Sense of belonging has been consistently linked to a wide range of student outcomes such as engagement, mental health and wellbeing and academic achievement. However, belonging is not experienced equitably across student groups, and the literature predominantly treats belonging as static.  This qualitative project aims to understand how different groups of students make sense of belonging at university and how they experience belonging. As the project is guided by an intersectional lens, together with exploring the dynamic nature of sense of belonging, there is scope for each student to personalise the project by identifying a specific (intersectional) group to focus on guided by a wide range of demographic factors such as ethnicity, gender, neurodiversity, caring responsibilities, disabilities, level of study etc. Students can also choose the methods they will use including traditional (e.g. semi-structured interviews; focus groups etc), and creative qualitative approaches (e.g. storytelling).
