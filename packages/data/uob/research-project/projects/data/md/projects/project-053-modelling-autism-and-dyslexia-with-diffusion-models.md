## Project title

Modelling autism and dyslexia with diffusion models

## Project summary

Recently a series of publications have examined the utility of diffusion models for understanding autism and dyslexia. However, the success of these models are mixed. A possible reason for these results is that these models oversimplify the processes involved in perceptual decision making. This project aims at testing this hypothesis by fitting a more detailed model to existing data.
