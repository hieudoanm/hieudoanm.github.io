## Project title

Multimodal imaging of neural representations in action planning

## Project summary

How does motor and premotor cortex, striatum and hippocampus coordinate the neuronal code for motor sequence planning? Synchronized oscillations are fundamental to brain function and are altered in neurological patients. Several cortical areas are associated with motor sequence planning, and there is regional dissociation of functional roles (Yewbrey 2023). However, the computational contribution of oscillations in key cortical and subcortical areas to skilled action control remains debated.
This project will localise – both temporally and spatially – the cortical and subcortical oscillations during motor sequence planning. This will allow for the precise targeting of neuromodulation to modify and enhance the recall and execution of skilled movements. The project will involve the collection and analysis of multimodal data in order to answer questions about the neural control of skilled movements.
