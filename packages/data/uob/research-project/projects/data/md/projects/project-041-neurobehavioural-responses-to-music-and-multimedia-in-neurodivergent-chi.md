## Project title

Neurobehavioural Responses to Music and Multimedia in Neurodivergent Children

## Project summary

This project asks why sensory-based interventions using music and audiovisual (AV) materials yield mixed outcomes in Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND) settings. Working with primary pupils aged 6–11 (with and without SEND, including ASD/ADHD), we will combine EEG, eye-tracking, and behavioral measures during structured tasks that manipulate melody, rhythm, harmony, and AV synchrony. By mapping how different learner profiles respond across perceptual, attentional, and control stages, we will distinguish when music/AV input supports engagement versus triggers sensory overload. The findings will inform inclusive pedagogy, guide the design of adaptive multimedia/assistive tools, and help create learning environments that respect sensory diversity.
