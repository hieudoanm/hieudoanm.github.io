## Project title

Do OPMs tell us more about brain activity than EEG?

## Project summary

Neuroimaging technologies are progressing quickly at the moment, Optically Pumped Magnetometers are an exciting new technology for Magnetoencephalography that promises to revolutionise our understanding of the brain. However, it is challenging to compare to existing methods like EEG and we often end up just making indirect comparisons or relying on assumptions that OPMs are better 'in theory'.

This project will work with a rare dataset that acquired simultaneous EEG-OPM from participants during a variety of tasks. This will allow us to track the information recorded by each modality from identical underlying brain sources. Analysis of this dataset allows us to compare what aspects of brain function are captured by each. We can use this to quantify the benefit of emerging OPM technologies for quantifying critical but challenging features such as bursting dynamics and waveform shape.

There is flexibility about the specific analysis and members of the group can collaborate on data processing but work towards personal goals, I'm happy to discuss specific ideas or pitches prior to the project - just get in touch.

This is a data-analysis focused project that will make use of existing open-access data though there may be opportunities to gain exposure to the EEG and OPM labs. This project will have proportionally more time for analysis and coding than projects involving data collection and there is an expectation that our analysis will be explored in considerable detail.

The project will suit people looking to strengthen their understanding of neuronal oscillations, data-science, statistics and Python coding. Some prior experience and interest in these topics will be required.
