## Project summary

We have been developing the Fringe-P3 Method for many years now. It involves rapid serial visual presentation (RSVP) of stimuli and detection of the P3 brain response. The method is based upon the finding that only salient stimuli presented in RSVP evoke a P3. In this way, the Fringe-P3 method can be used to determine what a participant finds salient, potentially if the individual being tested does or does not want this information to be known. This method has been proposed as a concealed knowledge test, as a means to perform police line-ups and as an objective visual acuity test.
