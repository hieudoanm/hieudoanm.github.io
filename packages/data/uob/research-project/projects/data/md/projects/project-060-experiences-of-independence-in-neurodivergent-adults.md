## Project title

Experiences of independence in neurodivergent adults.

## Project summary

By conventional metrics, neurodivergent adults are often considered to be less independent than their neurotypical peers. Such metrics draw on neuronormative frameworks and criteria for success. More recently, stress has moved towards more neuro-affirmative approaches, aimed at helping neurodivergent adults to flourish, rather than correct areas of difficulty. Such approaches require a reappraisal of how neurodivergent people themselves conceptualises their strengths and needs, their priorities and fears. With respect to independence, we (Bhattacharya et al., 2026) have recently conducted qualititative work to understand how autistic adults make sense of their experience of adults. This project will extend that work to consider the experiences of other neurodivergent groups - such as those with ADHD or dyslexia.
