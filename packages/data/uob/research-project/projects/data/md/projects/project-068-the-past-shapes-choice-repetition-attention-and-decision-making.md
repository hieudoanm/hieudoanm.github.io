## Project title

The Past Shapes Choice: Repetition, Attention, and Decision Making

## Project summary

Attention is an essential cognitive mechanism that has an important role in decision making – you will always choose the option you have paid attention to, and never one you haven’t attended. This project will seek to understand how repetition affects decision making via attention. Participants perform a simple visual search task. We will investigate how past experiences affect decision making via an attentional manipulation.
