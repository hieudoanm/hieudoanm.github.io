## Project title

How can GenAI support the development of critical thinking skills in learners?

## Project summary

The advent of Large Language Models (LLMs) presents significant challenges for education. Early evidence suggests that their use may have a detrimental impact on students' learning. However,  LLM's responses can be shaped through careful prompt engineering. This project aims to explore how prompts can be designed, so that they can support the development of students' critical thinking skills, rather than hinder them.
