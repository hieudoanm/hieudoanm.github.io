## Project title

Modelling of EEG data from a flanker study

## Project summary

This research project aims at advancing our understanding of how perceptual noise influences visual selective attention and to develop a link between EEG-signals and computational models of human behaviours. In Deakin and Heinke (2023) you find details on the background of the project and results from behavioral experiments.  Based on this work we recently collected EEG data from 50 participants. The MSc project aims at analyzing this data using standard EEG methodology and novel modelling methods following up Deakin and Heinke (2023).
