## Project title

Watching neural competition unfold in the real world

## Project summary

How does the brain decide what deserves attention in a cluttered world? Using an existing magnetoencephalographic dataset, we will track neural competition millisecond by millisecond as people attend to objects in natural scenes. Contemporary AI vision models will quantify each object’s representational geometry and strength, allowing us to test how AI-derived representational pressure drives attentional selection.
