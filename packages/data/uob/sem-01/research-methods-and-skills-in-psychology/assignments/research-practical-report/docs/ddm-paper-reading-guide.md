**Purpose:** A navigation aid for reading the supplied review. These notes identify topics and questions to investigate; they are not report prose or ready-made arguments.

## Terms to look up in the article glossary

- Evidence accumulation and accumulator
- Drift rate
- Boundary separation / decision threshold
- Starting point
- Nondecision time
- Across-trial variability
- Fixed and collapsing boundaries
- Correct and error response-time distributions

## Questions for your own reading notes

- Which task types and research areas does the review cover?
- What kinds of behavioural data does the standard model attempt to account for?
- What does the review say each model component represents, and what assumptions does it make?
- Which alternatives or unresolved issues does the review compare, and what evidence does it use for that comparison?
- Which claims are specific to the standard two-choice model, and which concern extensions or alternatives?
- Which ideas from this review are relevant to the practical you completed, according to your practical lead’s instructions?

Use the article as a starting point, then check its cited primary studies and any newer sources relevant to the report question. The report brief does not impose a reference-count minimum, maximum, or publication-date cutoff.
