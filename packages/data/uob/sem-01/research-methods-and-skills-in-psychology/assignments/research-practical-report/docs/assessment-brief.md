# Research Practical Report assessment brief

**Module:** Research Methods and Skills in Psychology (38954)  
**Assessor:** Prof. Andrea Krott  
**Assessment:** Research Practical Report

This is a Markdown reference to the supplied _Assessment Brief 2026 practical report_. Follow the practical lead’s instructions for the specific practical: the brief says those instructions override its general guidance.

## Purpose and learning outcomes

The report assesses theoretical and practical understanding of a research method and its use in a practical investigation. The stated learning outcomes are to:

- Explain the theoretical principles underlying a research methodology.
- Critically evaluate its assumptions, reliability, validity, strengths, and limitations.
- Apply the method to psychological research questions.
- Describe and interpret research conducted in the practical.
- Integrate practical and theoretical methodological knowledge.
- Use and synthesise relevant scholarly literature.
- Communicate scientific information in a professionally presented report.

## Required report content

The report has two parts:

1. **Theoretical and practical description of the method — approximately 1,500 words.** Explain the method in depth, including its assumptions, validity and reliability (for example, measurement or analysis considerations), advantages, and limitations. Comparison with alternatives may be appropriate. The brief suggests self-reading from seed papers supplied by the practical tutor and asks students to follow tutor guidance.
2. **Description of work completed in the practical — approximately 500 words.** Describe the actual practical work. Depending on the practical and the lead’s instructions, this may include background and research question, methods, results, and discussion.

The organisation and specific content depend on the practical. Confirm the required sections and reporting details with the practical lead.

## Other required elements

- Include the cover sheet specified on Canvas.
- Choose a title that captures the practical’s topic or purpose.
- Identify the practical taken, either at the beginning of the report or after “assessment type” on the cover sheet.
- Include a consistently formatted reference list in the style specified by the practical lead. If no style is specified, the brief permits a professionally accepted style such as APA, Harvard, or IEEE.
- Number the pages.

## Writing and presentation

- Write in a scholarly, formal, evidence-based style for an educated Psychology or Neuroscience reader; explain specialist ideas as needed.
- Use accurate and inclusive language, concise sentences, and logical organisation.
- Keep formatting consistent, including typeface, double spacing, and heading levels.
- Follow the practical lead’s advice about whether to include an appendix.
- Use diagrams, tables, and figures when appropriate, but make the text understandable on its own.
- Do not report the same numerical or statistical result both in the prose and in a table.
- Proofread for typos and formatting inconsistencies.

## Word limit

The report must not exceed **2,000 words**. The count includes in-text citations and excludes references, figures, diagrams, tables, their captions, and their notes.

## Use of generative AI

The brief permits AI for brainstorming, creating structures, and generating ideas for improving work. It explicitly says **no AI-generated content is allowed in the final submission**. Its examples permit outlines and examples but say not to ask AI for content or critical arguments. Keep AI assistance within those assignment-specific limits and declare its use as required by the official form.
