# Assessment submission and GenAI form

**Source:** *LES Assessment submission and use of GenAI form 2026-27.docx*, University of Birmingham, College of Life and Environmental Sciences.

This is a Markdown reference to the form. Complete the official form and submit it with the report.

## Submission instructions and fields

For a written assignment, include the completed cover sheet as the first page. For other coursework, submit it as a separate document alongside the work. The form states that incomplete GenAI information will delay marking until the form is supplied.

The form requests:

- Student ID (the title page says not to write your name)
- Programme: undergraduate or postgraduate
- Module title and assessment type
- Report title and word count
- Yes/no permission for the work to be considered as an anonymised exemplar in the Bank of Assessed Work
- Reflection on previous feedback (maximum 60 words)
- Reflection on formative feedback, if received (maximum 60 words)
- GenAI type selection and reflection on AI use

For a specific feedback request, the form directs students to leave a comment on their Canvas submission.

## GenAI categories on the form

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| A | The assessment is designed so GenAI cannot make a meaningful contribution. |
| B | GenAI may support preparation and planning, but the assessed submission remains the student’s own work. |
| C | GenAI may contribute to the assessed output if its use is transparently declared in line with the assignment brief. |

The attached form leaves the category blank for completion. The practical report brief’s planning-only permission and ban on AI-generated final content correspond to the form’s Type B description. Follow any confirmation from the module team when completing the official form.

## Declaration details

- For Type B, name the GenAI platform or platforms used, outline how they supported preparation and planning, and reflect on how helpful the outputs were in informing the assignment.
- For Type C, identify which parts of the submitted work are AI-generated and name the platform or platforms used, following the assignment brief.

By submitting, the student confirms that AI use in the final submission follows the assignment guidance. The form warns that use outside the permitted scope breaches the University of Birmingham Code of Practice on Academic Integrity and may lead to an academic integrity investigation.
