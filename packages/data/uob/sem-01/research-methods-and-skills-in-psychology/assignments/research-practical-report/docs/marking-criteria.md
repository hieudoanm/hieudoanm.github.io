# Coursework marking criteria

**Source:** _Coursework Marking Criteria 26-27.docx_, supplied with the practical report materials. The source is the School of Psychology rubric, Version September 2025.

## How marking works

The same criteria apply to undergraduate and postgraduate work, with level differences expressed through the learning outcomes and assignment tasks. Marking is holistic: the assessor gives one overall mark, not a sum or average of criterion scores.

The criteria are:

1. Knowledge and understanding of the topic, including accurate terminology.
2. Analysis using psychological or neuroscientific principles, with explained critical thinking about why issues matter.
3. Relevant reading and accurate APA referencing.
4. Clear and coherent structure.
5. Standard of English and appropriate academic style.
6. Compliance with assignment-specific generative AI guidance. This criterion does not contribute to the grade.
