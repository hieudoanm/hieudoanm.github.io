## Abstract summaries

_Concise paraphrases of the cited works’ abstracts; these are summaries, not verbatim abstracts._

### 1. [Estimating across-trial variability parameters of the diffusion decision model: Expert advice and recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmp.2018.09.004)

Offers practical recommendations for estimating across-trial variability parameters, including cautions about estimation and model specification.

### 2. [Sequential sampling models in cognitive neuroscience: Advantages, applications, and extensions](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-122414-033645)

Reviews the role of sequential-sampling models in cognitive neuroscience, covering their explanatory advantages, applications, and extensions.

### 3. [Testing the drift-diffusion model](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2011446117)

Develops formal identification results and a statistical test to evaluate whether choice and response-time data fit the DDM.

### 4. [A practical introduction to using the drift diffusion model of decision-making in cognitive psychology, neuroscience, and health sciences](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.1039172)

Introduces DDM principles and walks readers through application, model validation, result interpretation, and reporting.

### 5. [Drift-diffusion cognitive models: Description, applications and perspectives](https://doi.org/10.1080/02109395.2022.2056802)

Surveys drift-diffusion cognitive models, their uses, and broader research perspectives.

### 6. [Diffusion decision model: Current issues and history](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2016.01.007)

Reviews the DDM’s history, central components, current debates, and extensions across decision-making research.

### 7. [Decision making on spatially continuous scales](https://doi.org/10.1037/rev0000117)

Extends diffusion modelling to continuous spatial judgments and evaluates how accumulated evidence can produce a spatial response.

### 8. [Systematic parameter reviews in cognitive modeling: Towards a robust and cumulative characterization of psychological processes in the diffusion decision model](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.608287)

Synthesizes reported DDM parameter estimates to characterize plausible parameter distributions and support cumulative modelling.

### 9. [The EZ diffusion model provides a powerful test of simple empirical effects](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-016-1081-y)

Simulations show that the simpler EZ model can detect some effects more powerfully than the full DDM, despite data being generated by the full model.

### 10. [Testing the validity of conflict drift-diffusion models for use in estimating cognitive processes: A parameter-recovery study](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-017-1271-2)

Tests three conflict DDMs under different fitting methods and trial counts; recovery is limited in some conditions, but model constraints can improve validity, and the authors give practical recommendations for use.
