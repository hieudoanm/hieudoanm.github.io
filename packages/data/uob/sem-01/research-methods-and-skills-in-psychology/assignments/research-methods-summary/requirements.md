# Research Method Summary

## Module Information

- **Module Name:** Research Methods and Skills in Psychology
- **Module Code:** 38954
- **Module Assessor:** Prof. Andrea Krott
- **Assessment Name:** Research Method Summary

## Overview

The Research Method Summary is a preparatory assessment designed to support you in completing the main assignment for this module, the **Research Practical Report**. The task provides an opportunity to develop your understanding of the research method used in your allocated research practical and to practise communicating methodological concepts clearly and accurately.

## Assessment Task

Write a **500-word Research Method Summary** based on your allocated research practical (e.g., Computational Methods, Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA), Factorial Design, or Standardised Tests).

Your summary should explain the basic principles of the assigned method in a way that would be understandable to a lay audience. Imagine you are explaining the method to someone with no prior training in psychology or research methods.

## Learning Objectives

This assessment aims to help you:

- Develop a clear understanding of the research method used in your practical.
- Demonstrate your ability to explain methodological concepts accurately.
- Practise translating technical information into accessible language.
- Prepare for the theoretical component of the Research Practical Report.

## What to Include

Your summary should address the following questions:

### 1. What is the method?

- Define and introduce the method in simple terms.

### 2. How does the method work?

- Describe the basic processes or procedures involved.
- Focus on the key ideas rather than technical details.

### 3. What can the method be used for?

- Explain the types of research questions or problems the method can help address.
- Provide examples where appropriate.

### 4. Why is the method useful?

- Briefly discuss its strengths or advantages.

### 5. What are the method's main limitation(s)?

- Briefly discuss its limitations and disadvantages.

## Writing Style

Your summary should:

- Be written in clear, accessible language.
- Be understandable to a non-specialist audience.
- Avoid unnecessary technical terminology and jargon. If needed, explain these.
- Demonstrate understanding rather than simply describing procedures.
- Be well organised and logically structured.

## References

You must support your work with appropriate in-text academic references.

- Use scholarly sources, such as textbooks and peer-reviewed journal articles.
- Cite all sources used within the text (and not more).
- Provide a reference list in an official referencing style.
- Ask your practical lead whether they prefer a particular reference style. If they have no preference, you can use [APA Style](https://apastyle.apa.org/).

### Referencing Software

- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)
- [Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/)

## Word Limit

Your report must **not exceed 500 words**.

- The word count excludes references.
- The word count includes in-text citations.

## Feedback on Your Work

You will not receive a grade for this assignment. However, you will receive feedback on:

- Accuracy and understanding of the method
- Clarity and accessibility of explanations
- Appropriate use of evidence and references
- Organisation and structure
- Appropriateness of language
- Appropriateness of the use of generative AI

> Note: You do not need to use generative AI.

The feedback will help you improve your ability to communicate research methods effectively in written coursework.
