# Assessment Submission and GenAI Form 2026–27

**Source:** University of Birmingham, College of Life and Environmental Sciences, _LES Assessment submission and use of GenAI form 2026–27_.

This Markdown version records the form’s fields and guidance. It is a reference; complete and submit the official form with the coursework.

## Submission instructions

- Complete all sections.
- For a written assignment, include the completed form as the first page of the submitted work.
- For other coursework formats, such as slides, posters, or video, submit the form as a separate document alongside the coursework.
- If the GenAI form is incomplete, the work will not be marked until the completed form is supplied.

## Assessment submission details

The form asks for:

- Student ID
- Programme: undergraduate or postgraduate
- Module title
- Assessment type
- Report title
- Word count
- Whether the student agrees to the assignment being considered as an anonymised exemplar in the Bank of Assessed Work: yes or no

## Feedback reflections

### Reflection on previous feedback

Maximum 60 words. Briefly describe something from earlier feedback that you tried to implement in the current assignment.

### Reflection on formative feedback

Maximum 60 words. If formative feedback was received, briefly explain how it was addressed in the final assignment.

If there is a particular area where feedback is wanted, the form directs the student to leave a comment on the Canvas submission.

## Generative AI use

The form asks the student to select the assessment’s permitted GenAI type:

| Type | Meaning in the form                                                                                                        |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A    | The assessment is designed so that GenAI cannot make a meaningful contribution.                                            |
| B    | GenAI may support preparation and planning, but the assessed submission remains the student’s own work.                    |
| C    | GenAI may contribute to the assessed output, provided its use is transparently declared in line with the assignment brief. |

The attached form leaves the assessment type blank for completion: **This assessment is Type \_\_\_\_**.

### What to disclose

- **Type B:** State which GenAI platform or platforms were used, briefly describe how they supported preparation and planning, and reflect on how helpful the outputs were in informing the assignment.
- **Type C:** Identify which parts of the submission are AI-generated and which platform or platforms were used. The declaration must follow the specific assignment brief.

The form states that submission confirms the student’s use of AI in the final work follows the relevant assignment guidance. It also warns that use outside the permitted scope breaches the University of Birmingham Code of Practice on Academic Integrity and may lead to an academic integrity investigation.

## Assignment-specific note

The Research Method Summary brief in [requirements.md](requirements.md) permits AI for brainstorming, structuring, and ideas for improving work, while prohibiting AI-generated content in the final submission. That guidance appears consistent with **Type B** as defined in this form. The attached form itself does not preselect a type, so use the type confirmed for the assignment by the module team when completing the official form.
