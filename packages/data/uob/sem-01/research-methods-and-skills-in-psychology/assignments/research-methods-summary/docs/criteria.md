# Coursework Marking Criteria

**Source:** _Essay Marking Criteria_, Version September 2025. This Markdown file reformats the School of Psychology criteria supplied in “Coursework Marking Criteria 26-27.docx.”

## How the criteria are used

The same criteria apply to undergraduate and postgraduate assignments. Differences between levels are reflected in the learning outcomes and assignment tasks. Markers assess the work holistically and assign one overall mark; the criteria are not added or averaged into a score.

The School evaluates work on:

1. Knowledge of the topic and accurate use of terminology.
2. Analysis using psychological or neuroscientific principles, including explained critical thinking about why the issues matter.
3. Relevant reading and accurate APA referencing.
4. Clear, coherent structure.
5. Standard of English and appropriate academic style.
6. Adherence to assignment-specific generative AI guidance. This criterion does **not** contribute to the grade.

## Mark bands

| Postgraduate band | Undergraduate class | Mark range | Mark steps          |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ---------: | ------------------- |
| Distinction       | First               |     70–100 | 72, 78, 85, 93, 100 |
| Merit             | Upper second (2.i)  |      60–69 | 62, 65, 68          |
| Pass              | Lower second (2.ii) |      50–59 | 52, 55, 58          |
| Marginal fail     | Third               |      40–49 | 42, 45, 48          |
| Fail              | Fail                |       0–39 | 0, 10, 20, 30, 35   |

The source also provides verbal labels for the undergraduate scale: outstanding, excellent, good, satisfactory, adequate, marginal fail, inadequate, and no submission. The postgraduate verbal labels are distinction, merit, pass, marginal fail, fail, and no submission.

## Performance descriptors

The table summaries below preserve the substance of the source descriptors. “Distinction” includes the source’s additional 80–100% standard where one is specified.

### Knowledge and understanding

| Band                  | Descriptor                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distinction / First   | Thorough, up-to-date knowledge and understanding, with reading beyond key texts. At 80–100%, also shows exceptional comprehension. |
| Merit / Upper second  | Relevant, sound knowledge and understanding of the topic.                                                                          |
| Pass / Lower second   | Relevant knowledge of the topic.                                                                                                   |
| Marginal fail / Third | Basic knowledge of the topic.                                                                                                      |
| Fail                  | Inadequate knowledge to meet the learning outcomes.                                                                                |

### Analysis

| Band                  | Descriptor                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distinction / First   | Resourceful, imaginative analysis using ideas beyond those introduced in the module; synthesises diverse sources; offers supported, considered critical evaluation and independent thought. At 80–100%, analysis is also sophisticated. |
| Merit / Upper second  | Analyses using ideas introduced in the module; shows some synthesis and specific, clearly explained, at least partly supported critical evaluation.                                                                                     |
| Pass / Lower second   | Mostly descriptive, with some discussion using module ideas and limited critical evaluation, which may be generic.                                                                                                                      |
| Marginal fail / Third | Mainly descriptive, with frequent unsupported claims or opinions; any critical evaluation is generic or unexplained.                                                                                                                    |
| Fail                  | Descriptive and uncritical; some discussion may be irrelevant to the task.                                                                                                                                                              |

### Reading and referencing

| Band                  | Descriptor                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distinction / First   | Wide, relevant reading and accurate, consistent APA referencing in text and in the reference list. At 80–100%, the work is fully supported by relevant, up-to-date material. |
| Merit / Upper second  | Informed by reading beyond key texts; APA is accurate, with possible minor lapses in detail.                                                                                 |
| Pass / Lower second   | Effective use of key reading; APA is accurate overall, with some lapses in detail.                                                                                           |
| Marginal fail / Third | Limited but appropriate reading; APA is largely accurate, though detail lapses occur and some claims may lack sufficient support.                                            |
| Fail                  | Poor use of reading and poor or incorrect APA referencing.                                                                                                                   |

### Essay structure

| Band                  | Descriptor                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distinction / First   | Clear structure that strengthens the argument and discussion; logical and easy to follow; introduces the aim in the opening paragraph and reaches a justified conclusion addressing the title. At 80–100%, the argument structure is particularly enriching.                    |
| Merit / Upper second  | Clear structure supporting coherent discussion and argument; basically logical, though it could be more persuasive. Introduces the aim, perhaps perfunctorily, and concludes from the main points, though the conclusion may not be fully justified or address the title fully. |
| Pass / Lower second   | Structure supports the discussion but is not always logical or clear. The aim may not be explicit or the introduction may be formulaic. The conclusion may omit aspects or introduce new ideas; the link to the title may be implicit.                                          |
| Marginal fail / Third | Some relevant structure, but the introduction is formulaic and does not address the aim; conclusion is generic or too brief and does not address the title.                                                                                                                     |
| Fail                  | Little evidence of planning; key issues may be omitted or repeated; introduction or conclusion is missing or irrelevant.                                                                                                                                                        |

### Use of language

| Band                  | Descriptor                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Distinction / First   | Excellent standard of written English.                                                              |
| Merit / Upper second  | Very good English, with all statements clearly expressed.                                           |
| Pass / Lower second   | Good standard of English with few grammatical errors and an appropriate academic style.             |
| Marginal fail / Third | Acceptable English, but errors may affect comprehension and the work may need more careful editing. |
| Fail                  | Poor English makes the work difficult to understand; register is inappropriate.                     |

### Generative AI guidance

This criterion does not contribute to the grade. In the Distinction / First descriptor, the cover sheet indicates adherence to the assignment-specific AI guidance. In the Fail descriptor, it indicates that the guidance was violated or the cover sheet was left blank. The source does not give separate descriptors for the middle bands.
