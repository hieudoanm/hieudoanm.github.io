# Research sources to review

> Working bibliography. These are research notes, not report prose.

## Dataset and measure documentation

- TalkBank. [CHILDES Clinical English ENNI Corpus](https://talkbank.org/childes/access/Clinical-Eng/ENNI.html). Corpus description, participant groups, task, CHAT file structure, and required corpus citations. The page states that the impaired group is best described as language impairment because IQ test information was not collected.
- TalkBank. [Mean Length of Communication Unit (MLCU) guidance](https://talkbank.org/childes/access/Clinical-Eng/ENNI-materials/Complexity/mlcu.pdf). ENNI-specific word-count and exclusion instructions. Read alongside the actual CHAT annotations before defining a reproducible MLCU measure.
- TalkBank. [CHAT manual](https://talkbank.org/0info/manuals/CHAT.pdf). Defines the `[+ bch]` postcode as an utterance-level exclusion marker; check the guide's specific use against ENNI transcript conventions.
- TalkBank. [ENNI materials index](https://talkbank.org/childes/access/Clinical-Eng/ENNI-materials/). Includes materials for Complexity, First Mentions, Stories, and Story Grammar.

## Corpus publications listed by TalkBank

- Schneider, P., Hayward, D., & Dubé, R. V. (2006). Storytelling from pictures using the Edmonton Narrative Norms Instrument. *Journal of Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology, 30*, 224–238. The TalkBank ENNI page lists this as a corpus reference; cite at least one listed corpus reference in any publication using the data.
- Schneider, P., & Hayward, D. (2010). Who does what to whom: Introduction of referents in children's storytelling from pictures. *Language, Speech, and Hearing Services in Schools, 41*, 459–473.

## Course textbook sections reviewed

- Olson, A. C. [Textbook repository](https://github.com/olsonac/textbook). Reviewed `permutation/permutation_idea.Rmd`, `permutation/permutation_and_t_test.Rmd`, `mean-slopes/Correlation.Rmd`, `mean-slopes/inference_on_slopes.Rmd`, and `mean-slopes/multiple_regression.Rmd`. These teach mean-difference and single-slope permutation examples, correlation, and additive multiple regression. The pages reviewed do not show a group-by-age interaction test.

## Review status

These sources establish corpus and scoring conventions but do not settle the group's research question or statistical method. Review the course's taught methods and the full MLCU criteria before finalizing the analysis plan.
