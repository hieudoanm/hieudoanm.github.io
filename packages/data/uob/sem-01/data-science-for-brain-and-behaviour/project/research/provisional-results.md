# Provisional analysis results

> Internal results for review only. The scoring algorithm is an approximation and these are not final report values until the group checks the transcript exclusions.

## Candidate outcome

- Analysis sample: 360 children (75 SLI, 285 TD); one TD participant has no age.
- Candidate MLCU: eligible surface-word count divided by eligible child-turn/C-unit count, pooled over six story sections.
- Existing `[+ bch]` lines and explicit `+...` / `+/` break-offs were omitted. Common CHAT mazes, retracing, fillers, and non-speech event codes were removed with a heuristic main-tier parser.
- The parser does not reliably identify every question response, unrelated utterance, or story-ender. It does not reproduce CLAN exactly.

## Primary age association

- Pooled slope: **0.3793 candidate MLCU words per C-unit per year**.
- Permutation test: **p = 0.0001**, two-sided, 10,000 shuffles, seed `20261001`, plus-one correction. No simulated slope was at least as extreme in absolute value as the observed slope.
- The direct-question sensitivity rule removes every child turn immediately after an examiner question. It gives slope **0.3734**, p = **0.0001**, n = 360. This broad rule may remove story-elicitation responses and is only a diagnostic.

## Secondary group descriptions

- SLI: n = 75, descriptive age slope **0.4210** candidate MLCU words per C-unit per year; mean MLCU 6.08 (SD 1.28).
- TD: n = 285, descriptive age slope **0.3726** candidate MLCU words per C-unit per year; mean MLCU 7.26 (SD 1.16).
- These estimates do not test whether the group slopes differ. The formal slope-difference test remains unapproved/out of scope pending instructor guidance.
