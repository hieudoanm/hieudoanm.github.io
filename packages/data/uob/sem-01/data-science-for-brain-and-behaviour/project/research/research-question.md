## Proposed hierarchy

**Primary question:** Across the ENNI sample, is age associated with children's mean length of communication unit (MLCU) in picture-story narratives?

**Secondary question:** Does the age–MLCU slope differ between children in the SLI and TD groups?

The primary question matches the textbook's simple-slope permutation procedure. The secondary question is an extension: the reviewed textbook chapters do not show a direct test of the difference between two group slopes. Both questions remain conditional on confirming that MLCU can be derived consistently from the CHAT files.
