## Questions and word limits

### 1. Project contributions — maximum 150 words total

Briefly list what you did in each relevant project section. Bullet points are permitted. Contributions need not be listed under every section for every group member.

- **Defining the question:** [Record your own contribution.]
- **Finding, exploring, and preparing the data for analysis:** [Record your own contribution.]
- **Visualization:** [Include visualizations even if they do not appear in the final report, where relevant.]
- **Analysis:** [Record your own contribution.]

The 150-word limit applies across all four subsections combined.

### 2. Dataset strengths, weaknesses, and learning — 200 words

Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of the dataset for addressing the question you defined, and explain what you learned about the data while working with it.

### 3. Critical evaluation of results and conclusions — 200 words

Critically evaluate the results and conclusions. Identify the most important results, assess whether they answered the project's question, and explain what you would change, add, or extend—including whether better or additional data would improve understanding of the topic.
