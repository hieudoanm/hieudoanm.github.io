# Candidate bibliography for the reflective statement

These sources offer optional background on data organization, processing choices, reproducible research, visualization, and uncertainty. The brief specifies APA style but does not require a fixed number of citations. Select only sources relevant to the actual project, read them before citing, and do not use references as a substitute for reflection on your own dataset and work.

## Brief source notes

- **Broman and Woo (2018):** Practical recommendations for structuring spreadsheet data to reduce errors and make later analysis easier.
- **Franconeri et al. (2021):** Evidence-based review of how visual design affects comprehension, interpretation, and communication of data patterns and uncertainty.
- **Munafò et al. (2017):** Broad recommendations for improving transparency, reproducibility, and efficiency in scientific research.
- **Sacha et al. (2016):** Explains how uncertainty can propagate through visual analytics and affect awareness, trust, and decisions.
- **Steegen et al. (2016):** Shows how reasonable data-processing choices can influence results and describes multiverse analysis as a way to assess robustness transparently.
- **Wilson et al. (2017):** Accessible practices for data management, documented analysis workflows, collaboration, and computational reproducibility.
- **Panagiotidou and Vande Moere (2022):** Discusses uncertainty arising from data collection, storage, context, and assumptions, with implications for visualization.
