Overview

Tells you whether the store you're browsing runs on Shopify — and whether it's Plus — by inspecting the page it's already served you. No network calls, no third parties.

Shopify signals

Checks window.Shopify, Shopify checkout meta tags, cdn.shopify.com scripts, and /cart.js.

Plus detection

Spots Plus via checkout.shopify domains, the window.Shopify.checkout flag, and wallet meta tags.

Ask, get an answer

Answers a CHECK_SHOPIFY message with a simple verdict: Shopify, Shopify Plus, or neither.

One-shot, offline

Runs a single pass at document_idle, no observers, no network requests — nothing leaves your browser.
