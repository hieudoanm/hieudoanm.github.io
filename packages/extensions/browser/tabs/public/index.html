Overview

Redirects new tabs, home, and private-browsing windows to the hieudoanm home page, throws up a wall on distracting sites, hides ads and tracking requests, and screenshots any page without leaving it — stitching the full scroll on an OffscreenCanvas for preview, copy, or download from the popup.

New tab redirect

New tabs open straight to the hieudoanm home page; flip the toggle off anytime right from the popup.

Block distracting sites

Facebook, X, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, Netflix, Twitch, and more — visiting one stops on a focus wall you can toggle off.

Block ads & trackers

Hides ad banners and cancels requests to DoubleClick, Google Analytics, AppNexus, Outbrain, and Taboola — fully offline, toggleable from the popup.

One-click capture modes

Grab the current view, the full scrolling page, or a specific element — then pick PNG, JPEG, or WebP at quality 92 from the popup.

Full-page stitching

Scrolls and stitches every section onto an OffscreenCanvas in your chosen aspect, no lazy-loaded gaps.

Suggestion wheel

Spin to land on something better to do — read a book, stretch, breathe, or write one paragraph.

Restores the page

Scroll position is saved before capture and restored after, so nothing moves — and preview, copy, or download from the popup.
