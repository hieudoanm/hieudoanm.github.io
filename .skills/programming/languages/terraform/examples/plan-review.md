# Plan Review

Before approval, answer:

1. Is this the intended account, project, or subscription, region, backend, state, and workspace?
2. Are every deletion and replacement expected, reversible, and backed up where needed?
3. Do identity, network, encryption, logging, and retention changes meet policy?
4. Are provider aliases and cross-boundary references correct?
5. Are resource cost, quotas, and operational consequences understood?
6. Is the exact reviewed plan being applied through the approved identity and pipeline?

If the plan is stale or the target changes, discard approval and generate a new plan.
