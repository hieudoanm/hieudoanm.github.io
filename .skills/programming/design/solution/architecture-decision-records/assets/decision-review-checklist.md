# ADR Review Checklist

- [ ] Is this a consequential, cross-team, costly, or hard-to-reverse decision?
- [ ] Is the decision question specific and bounded?
- [ ] Are context, constraints, drivers, and assumptions explicit?
- [ ] Are credible alternatives and status quo considered?
- [ ] Does rationale follow from evidence and priorities?
- [ ] Are consequences, risks, and owners recorded?
- [ ] Is status current, with review triggers and links?
- [ ] Is sensitive information excluded?
