# Status and Lifecycle

Record the decision's actual state:

- **Proposed:** under review; not yet binding.
- **Accepted:** chosen and expected to guide work.
- **Rejected:** considered and not selected.
- **Deprecated:** no longer recommended, but may remain in use.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a linked later decision.

Review decisions when triggers occur: changed constraints, incidents, cost or scale thresholds, new platform capabilities, security findings, or ownership changes. Preserve prior records and link replacements. Do not let obsolete decisions look current.

Assign an owner for follow-up and review triggers. A decision with unresolved assumptions should state who will validate them and by when.
