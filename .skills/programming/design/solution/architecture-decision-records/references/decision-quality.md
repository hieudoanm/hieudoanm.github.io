# Decision Quality

Quality depends on process and evidence, not whether the outcome later succeeds.

- **Clarity:** one decision question and bounded scope.
- **Traceability:** rationale ties to requirements, constraints, and evidence.
- **Alternatives:** realistic options and status quo are considered.
- **Honesty:** assumptions, uncertainty, dissent, and consequences are visible.
- **Proportionality:** investigation effort matches risk and reversibility.
- **Actionability:** ownership, next steps, and triggers are defined.

Do not use an ADR to retroactively justify a decision or present a preferred tool as a requirement. Separate the decision from implementation tasks and approval records.
