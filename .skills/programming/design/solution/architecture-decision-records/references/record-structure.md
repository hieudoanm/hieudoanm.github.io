# Record Structure

A useful record is short enough to read and complete enough to explain the decision.

1. **Title:** decision stated as a concise proposition.
2. **Status and date:** proposed, accepted, rejected, deprecated, or superseded.
3. **Context:** problem, constraints, forces, quality needs, and assumptions.
4. **Decision drivers:** criteria that distinguish the options.
5. **Options:** credible choices, including the status quo where relevant.
6. **Decision and rationale:** what is chosen and why.
7. **Consequences:** benefits, costs, risks, mitigations, and follow-up.
8. **Links:** related designs, implementation, or prior records.

Use the [ADR template](../assets/adr-template.md). Keep implementation specifics in their own technical documentation unless they are part of the decision.
