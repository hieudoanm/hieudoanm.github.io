# Architecture Workflow

## Discover

Identify stakeholders, user journeys, business outcomes, regulatory obligations, existing systems, and decision authority. Review code, deployment, support responsibilities, and production evidence before proposing a target state.

## Define

Separate functional requirements from quality attributes. Convert vague needs such as “fast,” “secure,” or “highly available” into scenarios with workload, context, response, and measurable success criteria. Record assumptions and unresolved questions.

## Design

Model context, responsibilities, data ownership, critical flows, trust boundaries, failure handling, deployment, and operational ownership. Compare at least one viable alternative and the existing baseline.

## Validate and communicate

Test uncertain assumptions with prototypes, estimates, threat modeling, operational exercises, or stakeholder review. Publish the smallest set of views and decisions that lets teams build and operate the system. Revisit when assumptions or constraints change.
