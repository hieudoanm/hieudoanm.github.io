# Example: Managed Service Decision

**Illustrative decision; no vendor recommendation is made.**

A team considers a managed data service rather than operating its own. It evaluates required data location, availability, recovery objectives, access controls, portability, pricing under expected and peak use, service limits, and support escalation.

The design records provider responsibilities versus team responsibilities, failure modes, export and exit procedures, and an estimate range with assumptions. A small proof of concept checks the highest-risk integration and operational constraints before commitment.
