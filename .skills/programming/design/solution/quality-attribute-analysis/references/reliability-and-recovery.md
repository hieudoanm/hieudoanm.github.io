# Reliability and Recovery

Define service boundaries, critical functions, acceptable degradation, dependencies, failure assumptions, and recovery objectives. Availability percentages need a time window, measurement point, and scope. Recovery time and data-loss objectives require agreed definitions and evidence from restore tests.

Identify single points of failure, cascading failure paths, retry amplification, queue buildup, and operator dependencies. Plan graceful degradation, timeouts, idempotency, redundancy, backup, restore, and incident response where they address prioritized scenarios.

Resilience mechanisms introduce complexity and can fail together. Validate failure behavior with safe exercises and review real incident data; do not equate redundancy with resilience.
