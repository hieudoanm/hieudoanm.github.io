# Example: Recovery Objective

**Illustrative planning scenario; no recovery guarantee is implied.**

A business workflow depends on persistent records. Stakeholders define the maximum tolerable restoration time and data loss, affected scope, and operating window. Architecture maps backup frequency, replication, restore dependencies, and manual steps to those objectives.

The team proves the plan through restore exercises and records actual recovery times and data gaps. A configured backup schedule alone does not establish recoverability.
