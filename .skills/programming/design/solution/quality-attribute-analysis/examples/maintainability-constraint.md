# Example: Maintainability Constraint

**Illustrative constraint.**

A small team must safely change pricing rules without coordinating releases across unrelated product areas. Define observable outcomes such as independent ownership, bounded regression scope, and a representative change completed through the normal pipeline.

Compare modular boundaries, tests, deployment coupling, and team expertise. Avoid turning “maintainable” into a mandate for a specific pattern without evidence that the pattern reduces the stated change cost.
