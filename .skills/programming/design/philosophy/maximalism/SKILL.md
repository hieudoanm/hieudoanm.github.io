# Maximalism

Four philosophies in this directory form one map. Minimalism and maximalism vary **how much** is present; flat and brutalism vary **how finished** it looks.

Maximalism is **deliberate density and expression in service of the content.** Where minimalism says _remove until the structure is clear_, maximalism says **add until the structure is felt** — pattern, saturation, layering, ceremony, scale. Density is the gift it offers the reader who wants everything.

## When to use

Use when creating or reviewing an interface that should follow Maximalism design guidance.

## Prerequisites

- A clear product or design goal.
- Familiarity with the target audience and existing interface constraints.

## Scope boundary

- When the brief does not call for this design system or philosophy; follow the project’s existing design language instead.

## Essential checks

- **Ornament is structure** — decoration that repeats becomes a visual grammar
- Filler decoration communicates nothing; patterned space communicates energy,
- place, and era
- **Systematic or nothing** — repetition plus alignment. The same motif applied
- consistently reads as language; applied randomly it reads as an error
- **Density as generosity** — a catalogue that shows everything rewards the reader
- who wants it. Hiding is the minimalism failure mode, and sometimes it is right
- **Scale contrast is the engine** — drama comes from extreme size differences
