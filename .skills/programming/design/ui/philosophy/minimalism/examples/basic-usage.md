Apply minimalism as a working method for web/app interfaces — what to subtract, in what order, and the floor you may not cut below. Covers the four distinct meanings of "minimal", the cut ledger, defaults over configuration, and when minimalism is the wrong goal. Use when asked to simplify or clean up UI, when a screen feels heavy or cluttered, or when reviewing whether a reduction actually improved the interface.
