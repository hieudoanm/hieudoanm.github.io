# Flat Design

Four philosophies in this directory form one map. Minimalism and maximalism vary **how much** is present; flat and brutalism vary **how finished** it looks.

Flat rejects **depth**. Where minimalism says _remove until the structure is clear_, flat says **remove the renderer's illusions and keep the clarity**. Skewed panels, gradients, inner shadows, leather textures, and glass: gone. What replaces them is colour, a 1px border, and space.

## When to use

Use when creating or reviewing an interface that should follow Flat Design design guidance.

## Prerequisites

- A clear product or design goal.
- Familiarity with the target audience and existing interface constraints.

## Scope boundary

- When the brief does not call for this design system or philosophy; follow the project’s existing design language instead.

## Essential checks

- **No z-depth** — no shadows, gradients, bevels, gloss, inner shadows, or
- textures. The surface is the surface
- **Colour does the structural work** — solid blocks replace every depth cue
- This is the core move; everything else follows
- **Borders instead of elevation** — a 1px hairline, or nothing. Not both
- **Typography carries hierarchy** — not size alone, not effects, not icon scale
- Weight, colour, and spacing do the work
- **Consistency over character** — one radius, one stroke weight, one icon family,
