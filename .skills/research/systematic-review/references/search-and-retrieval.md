# Search and Retrieval

## Build a reproducible search

Translate question concepts into controlled vocabulary and free-text terms. Adapt syntax for each database; do not paste one query unchanged across platforms. Have an information specialist review complex searches when available.

Record for every source:

- Database or platform and coverage.
- Exact strategy as run, including filters and fields.
- Search date, interface, and result count.
- Export format, file name, and deduplication steps.
- Supplementary methods such as citation chasing or trial registries.

## Coverage and limits

Select sources that cover the relevant disciplines, designs, geographies, and publication types. Grey-literature or registry searches may reduce some publication gaps but require planned methods. Explain language, date, and publication-status restrictions.

## Updates

For updated searches, preserve the original strategy and identify changes, new dates, and deduplication against earlier results. Search results are time-sensitive; record the final search date and avoid calling it current indefinitely.
