# Screening and Extraction

## Screening

Convert eligibility criteria into operational rules. Pilot on a sample, resolve ambiguous cases, and record rule changes. Define reviewer count, independence, conflict resolution, and automation use. Automation may prioritize or assist records but requires documented human oversight and validation suitable to the task.

At full text, record a specific primary exclusion reason per excluded report when required. Link multiple reports to the same underlying study to avoid double counting.

## Extraction

Use a structured, piloted form. Capture citation, setting, design, participants, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome definitions, timing, sample, results, analysis, funding/conflicts, and limitations relevant to the question. Add domain-specific fields.

Record page/table/figure locations and extraction uncertainty. Define independent verification for critical values; do not silently resolve inconsistent reports. Contact authors only under a documented plan and record nonresponse.

## Data stewardship

Version forms and datasets, protect personal or restricted data, and retain transformations. Share only what licenses, copyright, consent, and governance permit.
