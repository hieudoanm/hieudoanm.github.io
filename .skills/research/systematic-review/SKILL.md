# Systematic Review

## Purpose

Provide an auditable answer to a focused question through a planned search, transparent study selection, structured extraction, appropriate appraisal, and evidence synthesis. Systematic methods improve reproducibility; they do not ensure that the evidence base is complete or unbiased.

## Distinguish review purposes

Choose a review type that matches the question and deliverable. A systematic review uses explicit, reproducible methods to answer a focused question. A scoping review maps concepts, evidence types, or gaps and may not appraise or synthesize effects. A meta-analysis is a statistical synthesis that may be one component of a systematic review, not a synonym for it.

## Workflow

1. Frame the question and select an appropriate review type.
2. Draft and, where appropriate, register a protocol before screening outcomes.
3. Define eligibility and source coverage; develop and pilot search strategies.
4. Search databases and other justified sources; save dates, strategies, and exports.
5. Deduplicate records and screen titles/abstracts and full texts using recorded rules.
6. Document exclusions and reasons at the appropriate stage.
7. Extract study characteristics, outcomes, and context using piloted forms.
8. Appraise risk of bias or methodological limitations with tools matched to design.
9. Synthesize using a method suited to the evidence; pool only when estimands are meaningfully compatible.
10. Assess certainty, limitations, missing evidence, and applicability.
11. Report methods, flow, results, deviations, and supporting materials using current applicable guidance.

## Team process

Specify reviewer roles, independent duplicate stages where required, conflict resolution, calibration, and software. Do not imply duplicate independent review when it was not done. Pilot forms and screening rules on a sample, record changes, and keep decision logs.

## Synthesis and certainty

Separate study-level findings from review-level conclusions. Investigate heterogeneity and avoid vote counting by statistical significance. Use [literature-review](../literature-review/SKILL.md) for broader narrative synthesis and [meta-analysis](../meta-analysis/SKILL.md) only where pooling assumptions and outcome compatibility are defensible.

## Reporting and integrity

Use current reporting guidance appropriate to review type and field. Report protocol registration, amendments, search limits, inaccessible reports, exclusions, and deviations. Do not claim exhaustive retrieval unless the methods justify it. Keep search strategies and extraction data available subject to copyright, privacy, and licensing constraints.

## Completion checks

- Question, review type, protocol, eligibility, and source coverage are clear.
- Searches and selection decisions are reproducible and traceable.
- Extraction and appraisal methods fit the included evidence.
- Synthesis method and certainty judgments are explained.
- Reporting guidance, limitations, and any deviations are addressed.
