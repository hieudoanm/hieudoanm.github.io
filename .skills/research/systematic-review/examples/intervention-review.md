# Example: Intervention Review

**Illustrative protocol sketch; no search results or treatment effects are asserted.**

## Question

For adults with a specified condition, what are the benefits and harms of a defined intervention compared with usual care on a prespecified patient-important outcome at a defined follow-up?

## Plan

- Define condition, intervention variants, comparator, outcome measures, follow-up, and eligible study designs.
- Search bibliographic databases and trial registries with a peer-reviewed strategy.
- Screen independently as specified in the protocol; pilot criteria first.
- Link multiple reports to studies and extract outcome-level data with provenance.
- Appraise risk of bias using a design-appropriate tool.
- Pool only outcomes with sufficiently compatible definitions, populations, and estimands; otherwise narratively synthesize.
- Report certainty and applicability separately from statistical significance.

Before execution, identify current reporting and certainty methods applicable to the field.
