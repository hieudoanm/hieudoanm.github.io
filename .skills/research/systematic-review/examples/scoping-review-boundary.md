# Example: When a Scoping Review Fits Better

**Illustrative distinction, not a completed review.**

A team asks: “What digital tools, populations, outcomes, and implementation barriers have been studied for remote rehabilitation?”

This is a mapping question. A scoping review may chart concepts, evidence types, and gaps without estimating whether the tools work. The protocol still defines sources, eligibility, screening, charting, and reporting.

If the question changes to “Does tool X improve outcome Y versus usual care?”, use a focused systematic review with comparative eligibility and outcome appraisal. Do not present a map of the field as an effectiveness conclusion.
