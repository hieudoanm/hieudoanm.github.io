# Example: Rapid Review Adaptation

**Illustrative constraints record; not a recommended shortcut recipe.**

When a decision deadline is fixed, document each efficiency adaptation before searching:

| Adaptation | Reason | Likely consequence | Mitigation / disclosure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restrict sources | Deadline | Relevant studies may be missed | Justify coverage; search references |
| Single screener plus verification | Limited staff | More selection error | Verify exclusions or a defined sample |
| Narrow years/languages | Feasibility | Reduced coverage | Explain rationale and impact |

Call the work a rapid review if methods are streamlined. Do not imply comprehensive systematic coverage when restrictions materially limit retrieval or duplicate review.
