# Example: Sensitive Participant Data

**Illustrative plan; not approval to collect or store real participant data.**

A study collects interview recordings and health information. Before recruitment, the team obtains the required ethics determination, limits collection to necessary fields, stores files on approved encrypted institutional storage, and restricts access to named staff.

Contact details and linkage keys are held separately from transcripts. A documented process removes direct identifiers while reviewing quotations for indirect disclosure. Consent language specifies retention and sharing; no raw recordings are placed in public repositories.

The team records retention and deletion dates, access changes, and a response process for incidents. Local governance requirements determine the actual controls.
