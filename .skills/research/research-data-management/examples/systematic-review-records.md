# Example: Systematic-Review Records

**Illustrative records-management pattern; no study data are included.**

Store the exact search strategies, dates, exports, deduplication log, screening decisions, extraction forms, and analysis files under versioned project documentation. Assign stable record and study IDs so multiple reports can be linked without double counting.

Limit access to licensed full texts according to subscription and copyright terms. Share search strategies, aggregate results, and permitted extraction data; review licenses and publisher terms before distributing article PDFs or substantial copyrighted content.
