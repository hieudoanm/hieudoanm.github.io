# Preservation and Sharing

Decide what to retain, for how long, where it will be preserved, and who may access it. Follow consent, ethics approvals, institutional rules, funder conditions, agreements, copyright, and applicable law.

## Prepare a release

Include data and code only when authorized. Provide documentation, data dictionary, provenance, formats, license or access terms, citation guidance, and known limitations. Remove unnecessary identifiers and review disclosure risk. Validate that metadata and files remain consistent.

## Open or controlled access

Open data are not always appropriate. Use controlled access or share metadata only when sensitivity, consent, or rights restrict release. State the application process and conditions; do not promise future access that cannot be maintained.

## Preservation and deletion

Select a repository with suitable disciplinary fit, persistent identifiers, preservation policy, access controls, and sustainability. Set retention and deletion dates, including backups and working copies. Document what cannot be retained and why.
