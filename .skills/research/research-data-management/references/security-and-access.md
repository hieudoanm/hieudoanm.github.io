# Security and Access

## Classify before storing

Identify direct and indirect identifiers, sensitive attributes, contractual limits, consent conditions, and re-identification risks. Consult the responsible privacy, ethics, or data-governance office. Do not infer that publicly available data are unrestricted.

## Apply controls

- Use institutionally approved storage and transfer mechanisms.
- Grant least-privilege access; review collaborators and remove access when roles end.
- Separate identifiers and linkage keys from research content.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest where required by policy.
- Keep credentials outside source files and repositories.
- Define incident reporting, recovery, and secure deletion processes.

Pseudonymization is not anonymization. Data linkage or rare combinations of attributes can re-identify people even after direct identifiers are removed. Use a documented risk assessment before sharing.
