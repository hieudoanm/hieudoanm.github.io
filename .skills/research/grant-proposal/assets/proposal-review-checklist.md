# Proposal Review Checklist

- [ ] Current funder call, criteria, format, and eligibility verified.
- [ ] Problem and gap are supported by accurate, relevant evidence.
- [ ] Aims are clear, linked, feasible, and assessable.
- [ ] Design, outcomes, analysis, ethics, and data management support the aims.
- [ ] Team roles, access, facilities, and collaborations are confirmed.
- [ ] Risks, alternatives, timeline, and dependencies are credible.
- [ ] Impact pathway distinguishes project outputs from longer-term outcomes.
- [ ] Budget is accurate, eligible, justified, and institutionally reviewed.
- [ ] Claims, preliminary data, and commitments are verified.
- [ ] Proposal follows page, attachment, and submission rules.
