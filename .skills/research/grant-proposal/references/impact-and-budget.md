# Impact and Budget

Describe impact as a plausible pathway, not a guaranteed outcome. Identify users or beneficiaries, outputs, steps needed for uptake, assumptions, barriers, and how contribution could be assessed. Distinguish the project's direct deliverables from long-term societal effects that depend on others.

Build the budget from the work plan and current scheme rules. Tie each cost to an aim, task, period, and responsible person. Explain personnel effort, equipment, participant costs, travel, data stewardship, accessibility, dissemination, and indirect costs as applicable.

Check arithmetic, institutional rates, eligibility, quotations, currency, and justification with the authorized budget office. Do not invent cost commitments or hide material costs outside the proposed budget.
