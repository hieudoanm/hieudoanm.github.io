# Funder Fit and Criteria

Read the current call, scheme guidance, eligibility rules, scoring rubric, templates, budget policy, and institutional deadlines. Record the source and version of each instruction. Do not rely on last year's call or another program's norms.

Map each criterion to proposal evidence: where it is addressed, who owns the section, and what would convince a reviewer. Check investigator eligibility, geography, award duration, allowed costs, indirect costs, data or open-science requirements, and required approvals.

Decide whether the project genuinely fits the scheme. If the scientific question, career stage, scale, or impact model does not align, select a better call rather than reshaping claims beyond the evidence.
