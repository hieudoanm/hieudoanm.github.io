# Example: Specific Aims Page

**Illustrative structure only; no real proposal or preliminary result is represented.**

1. **Problem:** State the unmet need and who is affected, supported by verifiable evidence.
2. **Gap:** Identify what current evidence cannot answer.
3. **Objective:** State the project's bounded purpose and central hypothesis or question.
4. **Aim 1:** Define the question, method, deliverable, and success criterion.
5. **Aim 2:** Explain how it complements or extends Aim 1 without an unjustified dependency.
6. **Impact:** Describe the knowledge or decision enabled, with realistic scope.

Keep each claim tied to evidence and adapt length and headings to the current funder template.
