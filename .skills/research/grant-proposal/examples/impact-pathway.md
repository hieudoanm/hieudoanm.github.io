# Example: Impact Pathway

**Illustrative pathway; no impact is guaranteed.**

```text
Research activity
    ↓
Validated measurement evidence
    ↓
Shared methods and findings with practitioners
    ↓
Potential adoption in service evaluation
    ↓
Possible longer-term contribution to improved decisions
```

State assumptions at each link: stakeholder engagement, implementation resources, fit to practice, and further validation. Define indicators for outputs and near-term uptake; do not claim downstream outcomes are attributable to the grant alone.
