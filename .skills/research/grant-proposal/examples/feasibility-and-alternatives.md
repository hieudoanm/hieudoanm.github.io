# Example: Feasibility and Alternatives

**Illustrative planning example, not a confirmed project commitment.**

An aim depends on recruiting participants through partner sites. The proposal documents which sites have confirmed access, expected recruitment windows, and who owns coordination. A milestone checks recruitment progress before expansion.

If recruitment is slower than planned, the contingency might extend recruitment within the award period or use another approved site with comparable eligibility. It should not quietly broaden eligibility or replace the primary outcome after data inspection. Each alternative requires governance review and a clear effect on interpretation.
