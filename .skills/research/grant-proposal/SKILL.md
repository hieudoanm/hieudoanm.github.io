# Grant Proposal

## Purpose

Build a credible, funder-responsive case for why the research matters, what it will do, how it can succeed, and what resources it requires. A proposal must fit the actual scheme; persuasive language cannot compensate for weak methods, unsupported impact, or unrealistic scope.

## Workflow

1. Verify scheme aims, eligibility, dates, required sections, page limits, review criteria, and budget rules from current funder materials.
2. Define the problem, evidence gap, beneficiaries, and why the proposed work is timely.
3. Write a concise central hypothesis or objective and a small set of linked, testable aims.
4. Align each aim with design, measures, analysis, milestones, risks, and alternatives.
5. Explain team capability, environment, partnerships, data access, and feasibility.
6. State outputs and plausible impact pathways with dependencies and limits.
7. Build a justified budget and resource narrative with institutional owners.
8. Review against criteria, obtain specialist feedback, and check consistency and compliance.

## Specific aims

Each aim should answer a necessary part of the central question, have a clear deliverable and success criterion, and fit the award period. Explain dependencies and what happens if an aim yields a null or infeasible result. Avoid making every aim depend on an unvalidated earlier result.

## Significance and impact

Connect the gap to a meaningful scientific or societal need, supported by evidence. Distinguish direct outputs, near-term outcomes, and longer-term impacts. State assumptions, beneficiaries, adoption requirements, and risks; do not promise benefits beyond the project's control.

## Approach and feasibility

Use [study-design-protocol](../study-design-protocol/SKILL.md) for protocol detail. Include design, sampling, measures, analysis, ethics, data stewardship, milestones, risks, alternatives, and decision points at the level the scheme requires. Align resources with the work and explain access to participants, equipment, data, or collaborators.

## Completion checks

- Proposal directly addresses current scheme criteria and format.
- Aims, methods, outputs, timeline, team, and budget form a coherent plan.
- Claims and preliminary evidence are traceable and appropriately qualified.
- Risks, ethics, data governance, and alternative paths are addressed.
- Impact claims distinguish plausible contribution from guaranteed outcomes.
