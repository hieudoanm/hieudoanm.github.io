# Analysis Plan Review Checklist

- [ ] Question, design, population, and unit of inference are explicit.
- [ ] Estimand, outcome, contrast, and time point are defined.
- [ ] Primary analysis is justified by the design and data structure.
- [ ] Covariates and transformations have a rationale independent of significance screening.
- [ ] Assumptions and diagnostics are specified.
- [ ] Exclusions, missingness, and analysis populations are addressed.
- [ ] Multiplicity, subgroup, and sensitivity analyses are planned.
- [ ] Confirmatory and exploratory analyses are distinguished.
- [ ] Effect estimates and uncertainty will be reported.
- [ ] Statistical or domain review is obtained where the analysis is specialized or high-stakes.
