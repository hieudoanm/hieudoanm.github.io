# Estimands and Outcomes

An estimand defines the target quantity before selecting a statistical model. Specify population, treatment or exposure conditions, outcome variable, handling of intercurrent events, and summary measure as appropriate to the field and design.

## Outcome definition

Define construct, instrument, scale, direction, timing, unit, and clinically or scientifically important threshold where available. Distinguish primary from secondary outcomes and explain why the primary measure answers the question.

## Contrasts and populations

State the comparison, target population, follow-up, and analysis population. For randomized studies, clarify whether the target is assignment, adherence, or another strategy. For observational studies, define the association or causal contrast and assumptions needed to identify it.

Do not choose an estimand because it gives a preferred result. If a target is not identified by available design or data, state that limit.
