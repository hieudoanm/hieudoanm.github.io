# Model Choice and Diagnostics

Choose a model that matches the outcome scale, sampling/design, dependence structure, and estimand. Document model formula, link or transformation, covariates and rationale, random or cluster effects, weights, offsets, and software.

## Assumptions

List assumptions that matter to interpretation and define diagnostics or sensitivity checks. Diagnostics can reveal incompatibility but do not prove assumptions true. Avoid selecting a model solely by a fit statistic or p-value.

## Dependence and clustering

Account for repeated measures, clusters, families, sites, or shared controls. Define the independent unit and the level at which uncertainty is estimated. Ensure sample-size rationale and degrees of freedom match the design.

## Robustness

Prespecify justified alternatives for influential observations, functional form, distribution, or covariate set. Distinguish planned robustness checks from post hoc exploration and report all material deviations.
