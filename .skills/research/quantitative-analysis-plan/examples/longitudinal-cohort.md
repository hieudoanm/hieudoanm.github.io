# Example: Longitudinal Cohort Analysis

**Illustrative association plan; no causal effect is implied.**

A cohort measures a defined exposure and outcome repeatedly. The plan states the time origin, eligible cohort, exposure window, outcome timing, target association, confounder rationale, repeated-measure correlation structure, censoring, and loss-to-follow-up handling.

Diagnostics assess functional form and influential observations. Sensitivity analyses examine plausible exposure definitions and unmeasured-confounding limits where feasible. Report how selection, measurement error, and missing follow-up limit interpretation. Describe adjusted associations as causal effects only if a justified causal design and assumptions support that interpretation.
