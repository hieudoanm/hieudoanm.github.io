# Example: Exploratory Analysis

**Illustrative transparency pattern; not a prespecified confirmatory analysis.**

After a primary analysis, researchers explore whether an association differs by age group. The report labels the subgroup definition and model as post hoc, explains why the question arose, reports interaction estimates with uncertainty, and avoids claiming a confirmed moderator.

The team records the analysis date, data-access status, code version, and all related subgroup checks. A future study may prespecify and independently test the hypothesis.
