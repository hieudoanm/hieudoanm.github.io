# Example: Randomized Trial Analysis Plan

**Illustrative plan; no results or sample-size claims are made.**

- **Question:** Effect of assignment to intervention versus comparator on a prespecified outcome at a fixed follow-up.
- **Estimand:** Define target population, assignment contrast, outcome, handling of discontinuation/rescue treatment, and summary measure.
- **Primary analysis:** Specify analysis population and model aligned to randomization and outcome scale; justify baseline covariates before outcome review.
- **Missingness:** Report by arm and reason; prespecify primary method and sensitivity analysis.
- **Multiplicity:** Name one primary outcome/time point and define secondary analyses.
- **Reporting:** Present group estimates, between-group contrast, uncertainty interval, protocol deviations, and harms where relevant.

The exact analysis depends on design, outcome, estimand, and protocol; this sketch is not a universal trial recipe.
