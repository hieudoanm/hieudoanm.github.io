# Sensitivity and Diagnostics

Diagnostics can reveal incompatibility with assumptions but rarely prove them. Check overlap/positivity, treatment or exposure measurement, model specification, influential observations, missingness, selection, and dependence appropriate to the design.

Use sensitivity analyses to examine plausible threats: alternative confounder sets, functional forms, negative controls, quantitative bias analysis for unmeasured confounding, alternative bandwidths or comparison groups, and outcome definitions. Explain the threat each analysis probes and avoid a large undisclosed search for favorable estimates.

Report effect estimates and uncertainty across planned analyses. A stable result under one set of alternatives does not establish that all identification assumptions hold.
