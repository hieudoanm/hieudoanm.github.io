# Causal Questions and Estimands

Define the hypothetical contrast before selecting a model. Specify target population, intervention or exposure strategies, comparator, outcome, follow-up, and population-level contrast. For longitudinal questions, define time zero, eligibility, treatment assignment, and follow-up consistently.

Clarify whether the target is an effect of assignment, treatment received, a sustained strategy, or a policy. State how discontinuation, switching, death, or other intercurrent events enter the question. Different estimands can be valid but answer different questions.

Distinguish the target quantity from the estimator: a regression coefficient is not automatically the causal estimand. If available data or design cannot identify the target, narrow the question or report an associational quantity instead.
