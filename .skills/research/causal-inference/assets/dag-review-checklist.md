# DAG Review Checklist

- [ ] Variables have clear definitions and temporal roles.
- [ ] Outcome, exposure/intervention, and selection processes are represented.
- [ ] Arrows reflect plausible causal knowledge, not correlations alone.
- [ ] Confounders, mediators, colliders, and candidate instruments are distinguished.
- [ ] Selection and missingness mechanisms are considered.
- [ ] The adjustment set follows from the stated graph and target estimand.
- [ ] Plausible alternative graphs and their implications are documented.
