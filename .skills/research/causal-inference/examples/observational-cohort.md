# Example: Observational Cohort

**Illustrative causal question; not empirical evidence.**

Researchers ask whether a defined exposure affects a later outcome in a cohort. They specify eligibility and time zero, exposure strategies, comparator, outcome, follow-up, and target estimand before modeling.

A DAG informed by subject-matter knowledge identifies candidate baseline confounders and possible mediators. The team assesses overlap, measurement quality, time-varying confounding, selection, and loss to follow-up. If key assumptions are implausible or unmeasured confounding is substantial, report an association or bounds rather than an unsupported causal claim.
