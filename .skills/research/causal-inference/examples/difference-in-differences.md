# Example: Difference-in-Differences

**Illustrative policy-evaluation sketch; no policy effect is claimed.**

A policy changes in one region but not a comparison region. The target is a change in an outcome attributable to policy exposure for a defined population and period.

The plan states the treated and comparison units, intervention timing, outcome, and aggregation. It examines pre-intervention trends and composition, anticipatory behavior, concurrent policies, spillovers, and uncertainty with the correct unit of assignment. Similar pre-trends support plausibility but do not prove the parallel-trends assumption.

Use event-time plots and sensitivity analyses as diagnostics, not as automatic validation.
