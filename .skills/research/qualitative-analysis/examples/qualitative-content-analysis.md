# Example: Qualitative Content Analysis

**Illustrative design only; categories and counts are not findings.**

A team studies public policy documents to understand how “accessibility” is described. Before coding, it defines the document corpus, time period, unit (for example, paragraph), and whether analysis concerns explicit terms, implied meanings, or both.

The codebook defines categories, inclusion/exclusion rules, and examples. Analysts pilot it, revise definitions, and preserve document context. Frequencies may describe the selected corpus, but do not establish how common an idea is across all policy or public discourse unless the sampling supports that inference.

Report document selection, analytic level, researcher interpretation, and limits of the corpus.
