# Example: Mixed-Methods Integration

**Illustrative example; no real dataset or outcome is described.**

A service evaluation finds variation in appointment attendance across sites. The qualitative phase interviews a purposive sample selected to illuminate contrasting patterns and asks how scheduling, transport, and communication shape attendance.

Analyze each dataset with its own methods. Integrate through a joint display that places quantitative patterns beside qualitative explanations and identifies convergence, expansion, and contradiction. Do not use a small interview sample to validate an estimated prevalence or explain every site difference.

Protect confidentiality when linking participant narratives to site-level quantitative records.
