# Example: Reflexive Thematic Analysis

**Hypothetical teaching example; not real interview data or a finding.**

## Question

How do first-year students describe seeking help when they struggle with a new course?

## Analytic sketch

The researcher reads transcripts repeatedly, memoing how their role as a former instructor shapes attention to “help-seeking.” Initial codes might include timing, perceived cost, peer support, and uncertainty about expectations. These are not final themes.

A candidate interpretive pattern could concern how students weigh the social risk of asking against the urgency of a task. The analyst checks variation across cases and searches for accounts that do not fit.

The final report would ground the interpretation in contextualized excerpts and explain the researcher's role. Theme labels and all example codes here are illustrative, not empirical claims.
