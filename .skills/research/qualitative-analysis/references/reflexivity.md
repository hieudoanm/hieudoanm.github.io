# Reflexivity

Reflexivity is ongoing consideration of how the researcher's position, assumptions, relationships, and decisions shape data generation and interpretation.

## Questions to revisit

- What prior beliefs or theoretical commitments shape the question?
- How did identity, role, power, or institutional setting affect participation?
- What did the researcher notice, overlook, or find surprising?
- Which analytic choices changed and why?
- How might another plausible perspective interpret the same material?

Use memos throughout the project rather than adding a generic positionality paragraph at the end. Describe relevant influence concretely and proportionately; do not imply that reflexivity removes bias or guarantees neutrality.

Team analysis can broaden interpretation, but agreement is not always the goal. Explain how discussion, differing perspectives, and decisions contributed to the analysis within the chosen methodology.
