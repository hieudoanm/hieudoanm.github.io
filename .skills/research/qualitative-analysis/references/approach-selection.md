# Qualitative Approach Selection

| Approach | Typical analytic aim | Useful caution |
|---|---|---|
| Reflexive thematic analysis | Interpret patterned meaning across a dataset | Researcher subjectivity is part of analysis, not noise to eliminate |
| Framework analysis | Compare cases and topics using a structured matrix | Framework must remain open to unanticipated material |
| Qualitative content analysis | Describe or interpret manifest/latent content | Define units, categories, and level of interpretation |
| Grounded theory | Develop an explanatory theory through iterative comparison | Requires coherent theoretical sampling and analytic procedures |
| Discourse analysis | Examine how language constructs meaning, identity, or power | Text and context, not only topic content, are central |

Approaches vary within traditions. Name the version or orientation used, its assumptions, and why it fits the question. Do not claim a method solely because a familiar coding technique was used.

Before coding, specify the unit of analysis, role of prior concepts, inductive/deductive balance, case focus, and intended output. Reporting checklists can support transparency but cannot choose an epistemology for the researcher.
