# Ethics and Governance

Ethical and governance requirements depend on jurisdiction, institution, participants, data, and intended use. Consult the responsible ethics board, data steward, and qualified local experts; this note is not legal advice.

## Before recruitment or access

- Determine whether ethics review, exemption, or another formal determination is required; do not self-declare exempt.
- Define consent or other lawful/ethical basis, participant burden, risks, benefits, and withdrawal process.
- Provide safeguards for vulnerable groups, incidental findings, distress, and adverse events where relevant.
- Minimize collected data and define access, security, retention, deletion, and sharing.
- Check data-use agreements, licenses, export controls, and third-party restrictions.

## Protocol documentation

Record the approving body, decision or reference number, approved protocol version, consent materials, responsible contacts, and conditions. Specify how amendments and safety events will be handled.

For secondary or public data, verify permitted use, identifiability, re-identification risk, and whether the planned linkage or analysis changes the ethical basis. Public availability does not automatically mean unrestricted use.

## Reporting

Describe oversight and consent accurately, including waivers or exemptions as determined by the relevant body. Never imply approval that has not been granted.
