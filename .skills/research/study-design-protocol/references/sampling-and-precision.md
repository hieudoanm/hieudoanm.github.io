# Sampling and Precision

Plan sampling and sample size around the intended inference, not a copied convention.

## Specify the sample

Record the target population, accessible population, sampling frame, recruitment route, eligibility criteria, unit of sampling, and expected exclusions or attrition. Explain who may be underrepresented and how that affects generalization.

## Quantitative sample-size rationale

State the primary outcome and estimand, analysis model, meaningful effect or precision target, expected variability or event rate, error-rate assumptions where relevant, clustering, repeated measures, attrition, and any multiplicity strategy. Cite the inputs and show the calculation or simulation method. Sensitivity analysis should reveal how conclusions depend on uncertain inputs.

Do not treat retrospective observed power as evidence that an estimate is precise. Report confidence or credible intervals and limitations. A larger sample does not fix biased measurement or a poor design.

## Qualitative sampling rationale

Explain why selected participants, cases, or documents can address the question. Describe variation sought, access, stopping logic, and how the team will assess informational sufficiency. Do not import statistical power language where it does not fit the analytic approach.

## Clustered and complex samples

Account for dependence from sites, households, classes, repeated observations, or sampling weights. State the unit of inference and methods for design effects, clustering, or weighting.
