# Example: Measurement Validation

**Illustrative protocol outline; not a validated instrument.**

## Question

Does a new short questionnaire measure a specified construct reliably and validly for a defined population and use?

## Design components

1. Define the construct, intended decisions, population, language, and setting.
2. Review existing measures and document why a new or adapted instrument is needed.
3. Gather content feedback from relevant experts and members of the target population.
4. Pilot comprehension, burden, response options, and administration.
5. Prespecify reliability and validity evidence appropriate to the construct and use.
6. Recruit a sample suited to the planned psychometric analyses and report uncertainty.
7. Evaluate performance across relevant groups without assuming measurement equivalence.

Validity is an accumulating argument about score interpretation and use, not a permanent property established by one statistic.
