# Example: Observational Cohort

**Illustrative scenario only.** No empirical data or causal result is implied.

## Question

Is a defined workplace exposure associated with a later health outcome among eligible workers over a specified follow-up period?

## Protocol elements

- Define exposure timing, dose, comparison group, outcome definition, and follow-up.
- State eligibility and recruitment sources; assess selection and loss to follow-up.
- Identify confounders using subject-matter reasoning before modeling.
- Specify measurement sources, linkage rules, missingness, and data-quality checks.
- Define a primary association measure and adjustment strategy.
- Plan sensitivity analyses for exposure misclassification and plausible unmeasured confounding.

## Causal language

Unless the design and assumptions support causal identification, describe an adjusted association rather than an effect caused by the exposure. Report residual confounding and limits to transportability.
