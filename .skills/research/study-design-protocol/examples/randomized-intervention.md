# Example: Randomized Intervention Protocol

**Illustrative scenario only.** This is a design sketch, not an approved protocol or power calculation.

## Question

Among adults receiving outpatient rehabilitation, does adding a six-week home practice program improve a prespecified functional outcome at eight weeks compared with usual care?

## Design sketch

- **Design:** Individually randomized, parallel-group trial.
- **Population:** Define clinical setting, diagnostic criteria, age range, and exclusions with clinicians and participant advisors.
- **Assignment:** Concealed random allocation; specify sequence generation and who can access it.
- **Intervention/comparator:** Manualize program dose and document usual care.
- **Primary outcome:** Select a validated functional measure and assessment time before enrollment.
- **Analysis:** Specify intention-to-treat estimand, model, missingness approach, and sensitivity checks in advance.

## Decisions still required

The team must justify recruitment feasibility, sample-size inputs, assessor masking, adherence measurement, adverse-event monitoring, ethics review, and registry. No numerical effect or sample size is asserted here.

## Interpretation boundary

The study can estimate the effect of assignment under its design and implementation. Generalization depends on participants, setting, adherence, and care context.
