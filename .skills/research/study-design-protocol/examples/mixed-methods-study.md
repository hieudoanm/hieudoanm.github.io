# Example: Mixed-Methods Study

**Illustrative design sketch; no real participants or results are described.**

## Question

How acceptable is a new service to intended users, and what contextual factors explain variation in uptake?

## Design

Use a sequential explanatory design: first estimate uptake and variation from a defined quantitative dataset, then conduct purposively sampled interviews to explore selected patterns.

## Integration plan

- Link interview sampling to prespecified quantitative patterns, not convenience alone.
- Use a shared construct map and document where qualitative and quantitative measures differ.
- Analyze each dataset with methods appropriate to it.
- Integrate results in a joint display and explain convergence, complementarity, and disagreement.
- Protect identities in linked datasets and obtain appropriate consent and governance.

The qualitative phase does not merely decorate the quantitative result; it addresses a distinct explanatory question.
