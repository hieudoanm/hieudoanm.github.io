# Protocol Review Checklist

Record evidence or reviewer notes; a checked box alone is not a review record.

## Question and design

- [ ] Question, target population, setting, and intended inference are explicit.
- [ ] Design can address the question; key assumptions and alternatives are described.
- [ ] Primary outcome or analytic aim is distinguished from secondary exploration.
- [ ] Sampling, recruitment, and sample-size rationale are defensible and feasible.

## Procedures and analysis

- [ ] Measures, timing, procedures, and data-quality controls are reproducible.
- [ ] Analysis, exclusions, missingness, multiplicity, and sensitivity plans are stated.
- [ ] Deviations and exploratory work will be labeled and logged.

## Ethics and governance

- [ ] Ethics determination, consent, safety, privacy, and data access are addressed.
- [ ] Responsibilities, resources, and milestones are realistic.
- [ ] Registration and reporting requirements have been checked.
