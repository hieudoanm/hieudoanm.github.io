# Consent and Participant Welfare

Consent is a process, not merely a signed form. Explain purpose, procedures, foreseeable risks and benefits, alternatives, confidentiality limits, data use, compensation, contacts, and withdrawal conditions in language accessible to the population.

Assess decision-making capacity, voluntariness, power imbalances, accessibility, translation, and appropriate assent or representative permission. Avoid coercion or undue influence; compensation should reflect burden without compromising voluntary choice.

Plan for distress, adverse events, incidental findings, safety escalation, and referrals where relevant. Explain limits to withdrawal after data are anonymized, aggregated, or published. Do not promise confidentiality that cannot be maintained.

Consent for participation, future data use, data sharing, and publication of identifiable material may require separate consideration. Follow approved documents and seek review before changing use.
