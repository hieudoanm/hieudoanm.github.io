# Integrity and Conflicts

Research integrity requires accurate records and reporting, appropriate methods, protection of participants and data, and honest treatment of uncertainty and inconvenient results.

Disclose financial and nonfinancial interests that could reasonably be perceived to affect design, analysis, interpretation, or reporting. Develop a management plan with the institution or funder when needed. Disclose funding and sponsor roles in protocol, analysis, publication, and access to results.

Maintain source data and analysis records, report protocol deviations, correct errors transparently, and preserve negative or inconclusive findings. Do not fabricate, falsify, plagiarize, manipulate images, inappropriately reuse text, or conceal relevant limitations.

For concerns about misconduct or unsafe practice, use the institution's confidential reporting and investigation channels; do not conduct an informal investigation that compromises evidence or confidentiality.
