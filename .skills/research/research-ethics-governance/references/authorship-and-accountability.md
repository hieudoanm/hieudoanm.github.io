# Authorship and Accountability

Agree early on expected contributions, authorship criteria, order principles, corresponding-author duties, and acknowledgments. Revisit agreements when roles change. Follow the field's and publisher's current authorship criteria; job title, seniority, funding, or supervision alone does not automatically establish authorship.

Record contributions using a recognized taxonomy when appropriate, while ensuring each author reviews and accepts responsibility for the work attributed to them. Acknowledge non-author contributions with permission where required.

Agree on data stewardship, analysis access, manuscript review timelines, and dispute resolution. Do not add honorary authors, omit qualifying contributors, or assign accountability to people who did not approve the work.
