# Oversight and Review

Identify the body authorized to determine whether ethics review, exemption, or another approval is required. Requirements may depend on jurisdiction, institution, funding, intervention, data source, and intended publication.

Provide accurate information about participants, recruitment, procedures, risks, data, consent, and dissemination. Do not begin activities requiring approval before receiving it. If the project changes, ask the authorized body whether an amendment or new review is needed.

Keep the approved protocol and consent version, decision letter or reference, conditions, renewal dates, and correspondence. A journal checklist or public-data status does not replace an institutional determination.

For animal, clinical, biosafety, dual-use, export, or other specialized research, consult the relevant qualified committee and current local rules.
