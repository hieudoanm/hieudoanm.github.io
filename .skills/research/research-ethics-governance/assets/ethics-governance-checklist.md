# Ethics and Governance Checklist

- [ ] Responsible committee or office identified; determination obtained before required activity.
- [ ] Protocol, recruitment, and consent materials match the approved version.
- [ ] Participant risks, burdens, benefits, accessibility, and support are addressed.
- [ ] Data uses, access, privacy, retention, and sharing are permitted.
- [ ] Conflicts, sponsor roles, team responsibilities, and authorship expectations are documented.
- [ ] Amendment, incident, complaint, withdrawal, and escalation procedures are known.
- [ ] Reporting accurately describes approval, consent, and deviations.
