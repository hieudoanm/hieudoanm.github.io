# Example: Authorship Disagreement

**Illustrative resolution process; no real contributors are described.**

Two collaborators disagree about author order after project responsibilities changed. They review the contribution record, agreed field criteria, and current manuscript contributions; discuss expectations with a neutral supervisor if needed.

The team documents a transparent decision, obtains agreement that listed authors approve the manuscript and accept appropriate accountability, and acknowledges other contributions accurately. If unresolved, they use institutional policy rather than retaliating or adding honorary authors.
