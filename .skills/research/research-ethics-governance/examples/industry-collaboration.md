# Example: Industry Collaboration

**Illustrative agreement checklist; not legal advice.**

Before a sponsored study, the team clarifies sponsor role in design, data access, analysis, publication review, and dissemination. It discloses financial interests, protects investigator independence, and agrees on publication timelines without allowing indefinite suppression of results.

The agreement addresses ownership, confidentiality, intellectual property, data security, and conflict management. Institutional contracting and ethics offices review terms before work begins.
