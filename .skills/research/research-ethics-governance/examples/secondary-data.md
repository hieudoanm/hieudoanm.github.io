# Example: Secondary Data Research

**Illustrative governance scenario; public availability alone does not authorize every reuse.**

A researcher proposes linking two datasets that are individually accessible. They check licenses, original consent scope, terms of use, re-identification risk, and whether linkage changes the sensitivity or required review.

The responsible governance office determines required oversight. The analysis minimizes variables, uses approved storage, limits access, and documents retention and disclosure controls. The researcher does not assume the source's public status permits redistribution of linked records.
