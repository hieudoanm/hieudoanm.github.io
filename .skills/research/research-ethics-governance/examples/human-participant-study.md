# Example: Human-Participant Interview Study

**Illustrative planning example; not an ethics determination or approved protocol.**

A team plans interviews about access to a sensitive service. Before recruitment, it submits a complete protocol to the authorized committee, prepares accessible consent materials, and explains privacy limits and withdrawal conditions.

The plan addresses distress, participant support, secure recording, transcript access, quotation disclosure risk, and retention. Recruitment staff are trained to avoid pressure, especially where participants depend on the service. The team waits for required approvals and uses only approved materials.
