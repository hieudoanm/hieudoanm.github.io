# Research Gap

Identify, validate, and formulate genuine research gaps by systematically mapping the literature, distinguishing established knowledge from uncertainty and disagreement, checking recent evidence, and turning defensible gaps into researchable questions.

Read the [skill guide](SKILL.md) first. The references provide supporting methods; examples demonstrate application; assets are reusable templates.
