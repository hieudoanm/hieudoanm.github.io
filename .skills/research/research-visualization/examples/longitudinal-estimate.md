# Example: Longitudinal Estimate

**Illustrative display plan; not study findings.**

Plot estimated outcome by prespecified time points with uncertainty intervals and sample counts. Use lines only when connecting the same participants or when the model estimates a continuous trajectory; otherwise the line may imply unobserved continuity.

Explain whether points are raw summaries or model-adjusted estimates, how repeated measures are handled, and how missing follow-up affects denominators. Keep the scale and follow-up window visible.
