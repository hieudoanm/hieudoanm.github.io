# Example: Distribution Display

**Illustrative plotting guidance; no underlying data are provided.**

To compare a skewed continuous outcome, show distributions with sample sizes and a clear measure of center. A violin or density plot can conceal small samples or depend on smoothing; consider overlaying observations or using a box/point-range display.

Choose bins or smoothing before interpreting patterns and document them. Do not use a smoothed density as if each curve were directly observed.
