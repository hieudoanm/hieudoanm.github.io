# Example: Group Comparison

**Illustrative specification; no empirical values are supplied.**

For two or more groups on a continuous outcome, consider showing individual observations plus group summaries and uncertainty, especially for modest sample sizes. State the analysis unit, group denominators, outcome units, and interval meaning. If the inferential question concerns a contrast, include the contrast estimate and uncertainty rather than asking readers to infer it from separate group bars.

Avoid implying a treatment effect if assignment was not randomized or the design does not identify a causal effect.
