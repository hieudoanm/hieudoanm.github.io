# Visual Integrity Checklist

- [ ] Values match the source data and analysis version.
- [ ] Axis labels, units, denominators, and sample sizes are clear.
- [ ] Interval or error-bar meaning is defined.
- [ ] Scale and baseline do not exaggerate or obscure the result.
- [ ] Transformations, exclusions, and smoothing are disclosed.
- [ ] Visual language does not overstate causality or generalizability.
- [ ] Colors are accessible and redundant encodings are used.
- [ ] Figure is readable at final publication size.
- [ ] Synthetic or placeholder content is unmistakably labeled.
