# Captions and Graphical Abstracts

## Captions

State what is plotted, population or sample, outcome and units, time point, group definitions, sample size, uncertainty definition, and any transformations or exclusions needed to interpret it. Define symbols and abbreviations; cite source data where required.

## Graphical abstracts

Reduce the study to a question, method, main finding, and limitation or context. Preserve the distinction between association and causation. Do not turn a complex evidence base into an unsupported single arrow or imply clinical advice not evaluated by the study.

Use only verified values and source assets with appropriate permissions. Label placeholders and synthetic examples conspicuously. Check that the abstract remains accurate when viewed without the manuscript.
