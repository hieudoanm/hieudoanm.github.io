# Chart Selection

Choose a chart based on the analytic question and data structure.

| Purpose | Possible form | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Compare categories | Dot, point-range, or bar chart | Keep a meaningful baseline; show uncertainty where relevant |
| Show distribution | Histogram, density, box, violin, or empirical cumulative plot | State binning, smoothing, and sample size |
| Show paired or repeated values | Connected points or trajectory plot | Preserve pairing and identify missing observations |
| Show association | Scatterplot with justified smooth/model | Avoid implying causality; reveal overplotting |
| Show time trend | Line or interval plot | State time intervals and whether values are observed or modeled |
| Show composition | Stacked bars or proportions | Keep denominators clear; avoid hard-to-compare segments |

Prefer simple encodings and direct labels. Avoid 3D, decorative effects, dual axes that encourage false comparisons, and chart junk. A table is often better for exact lookup.
