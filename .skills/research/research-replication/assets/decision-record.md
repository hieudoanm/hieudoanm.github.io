# Research Replication: Decision Record

Use this record when applying [Research Replication](../SKILL.md) to a concrete project decision.

## Context

Design, evaluate, and interpret independent replications of scientific findings by defining the target claim, preserving the essential research question, identifying meaningful sources of variation, preregistering hypotheses and analysis decisions, collecting new evidence, and determining whether the original finding generalises.

Use this skill when the goal is to determine whether an existing scientific finding holds when tested again using **new evidence**.
