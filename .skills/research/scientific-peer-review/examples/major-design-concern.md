# Example: Major Design Concern

**Hypothetical review wording; no actual manuscript is assessed.**

> The manuscript interprets the observed association as an intervention effect, but exposure and outcome are measured cross-sectionally. Temporality and reverse causation are therefore unresolved. Please revise causal language throughout and discuss what the design can and cannot establish. A causal estimate would require a stronger design or explicit, defensible identification assumptions not currently presented.

This comment identifies the inference problem and offers a remedy without prescribing a preferred result.
