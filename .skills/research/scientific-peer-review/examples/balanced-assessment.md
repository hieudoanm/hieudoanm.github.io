# Example: Balanced Overall Assessment

**Illustrative assessment only.**

> The question is relevant, and the use of a clearly defined population and transparent outcome measurement are strengths. The main concern is that the analysis and causal interpretation do not address plausible time-varying confounding. The manuscript may become suitable after the authors clarify the target estimand, temper causal claims, and report sensitivity analyses that are supported by the available data. Several presentation issues are minor and listed separately.

An overall recommendation should follow the venue's options and be consistent with the substantive comments.
