# Scientific Peer Review

Review a manuscript, protocol, or revision constructively and confidentially, evaluating whether claims follow from methods and evidence.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) first and follow the venue's instructions. Review is not copyediting or a demand that authors reach a preferred conclusion.
