# Review Criteria

Assess the work against its question and venue criteria rather than one universal checklist.

- **Question and contribution:** Is the question clear, relevant, and appropriately scoped?
- **Design and methods:** Can the design address the question? Are sampling, measures, procedures, and exclusions described?
- **Analysis and inference:** Are estimates, uncertainty, assumptions, multiplicity, and missingness handled transparently?
- **Results and claims:** Do conclusions follow from results and limitations? Are null or adverse findings reported?
- **Ethics and integrity:** Are oversight, consent, conflicts, and data practices described as required?
- **Reporting and reproducibility:** Are methods, materials, code, and data availability adequate and permitted?

Separate concerns that undermine validity from issues of clarity or preference. State when your expertise does not cover a method and suggest specialist review where appropriate.
