# Constructive Comments

A useful comment identifies the location or claim, explains the concern, states its consequence, and suggests a reasonable remedy or clarification.

Prefer:

> The primary outcome is introduced in the Results but is not identified in the protocol or Methods. Please clarify whether it was prespecified and report any outcome changes; this affects interpretation of selective reporting risk.

Avoid vague or personal comments such as “the statistics are wrong” or “the authors do not understand.” Distinguish required revisions from optional improvements. Do not request extensive new work unless it can address a material concern and is feasible within the study's design.

Prioritize a small number of major issues, then list concise minor corrections. Keep confidential editor-only comments distinct from comments intended for authors.
