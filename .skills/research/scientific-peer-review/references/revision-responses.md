# Revision Responses

## Author response

Answer each comment in order. Quote or summarize the comment, state the action taken, identify exact manuscript locations, and explain respectfully when a change is not made. Describe new analyses and their limitations; do not imply that a post hoc analysis was prespecified.

## Reviewer of revision

Map each response to the original concern and inspect the revised text, tables, or analysis. Mark concerns resolved, partly resolved, or unresolved with rationale. Avoid introducing unrelated new demands in later rounds unless new information materially changes the assessment.

Editors make the final decision. A reviewer recommendation is advice, not a guarantee of outcome.
