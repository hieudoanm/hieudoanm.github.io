# Ethics and Confidentiality

Treat manuscripts, reviewer identities, editorial correspondence, and unpublished data as confidential under the venue's policy. Do not share, quote, or reuse unpublished material. Do not upload it to external services or generative AI unless the venue and authorized parties explicitly permit that use.

Declare actual or perceived conflicts, including financial, professional, collaborative, or strongly adversarial interests. Decline or request guidance when impartial review is not feasible.

Assess whether ethics and consent reporting appears adequate for the work, but do not make claims about approval status beyond the manuscript. Raise concerns through the editor's confidential channel and follow institutional reporting processes for serious issues.
