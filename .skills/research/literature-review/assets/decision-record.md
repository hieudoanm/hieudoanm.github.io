## Context

Conduct rigorous literature reviews by searching, screening, comparing, synthesizing, and critically evaluating research evidence.

A literature review synthesizes a body of research to answer a defined question, establish what is known, evaluate the strength of the evidence, identify disagreements and limitations, and identify gaps that motivate future research.
